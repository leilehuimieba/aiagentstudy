## Key Evidence

The WSTG reporting section describes how penetration-test findings should be structured so that other stakeholders can understand severity, reproduce the issue, and validate remediation. It emphasizes concrete findings, reproducible steps, and evidence that supports verification.

## Practical Notes

This is a strong template source for designing a lightweight proof object rather than a prose-heavy final report.
