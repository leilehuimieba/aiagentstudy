## Core Takeaway

This research article from Anthropic presents findings from a survey of 1,260 quantitative social scientists fielded in early 2026, examining the adoption and impact of AI coding agents like Claude Code. The study finds that while 81% of respondents have used AI chatbots for research, only 20% regularly use coding agents. Adoption is highly uneven: economists (39%) and political scientists (25%) lead, while public health (6%) and education (4%) lag. Researchers with typically male names use coding agents at twice the rate of those with female names, and top university researchers are 40% more likely to adopt them. Coding agent users report starting more projects and posting more working papers and grant proposals, but not more journal submissions, suggesting productivity gains may be concentrated in early research stages. The authors caution these are descriptive, not causal, findings. Researchers are optimistic about AI boosting paper-writing productivity but less confident about positive effects on the broader field of social science.
