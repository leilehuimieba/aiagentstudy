## Core Takeaway

This article offers an immersive account of visiting the Rogfast tunnel project in Norway, which will become the world's deepest (390 meters below sea level) and longest (26.7 kilometers) subsea road tunnel. The author describes the drill-and-blast method used, the constant battle against seawater ingress through grouting, the complex geology involving phyllite and gneiss, and the massive logistics including ventilation shafts on Kvitsøy island. Personal experiences — from the safety briefing and rescue chambers to witnessing a controlled explosion — bring the engineering challenges to life. The piece also touches on risks like driver monotony and environmental impacts on lobster fishing, and contrasts Norway's achievements with Elon Musk's Boring Company. Ultimately, it celebrates the audacity and possibility of grand civil engineering.
