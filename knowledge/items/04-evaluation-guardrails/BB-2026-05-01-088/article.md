Replit's CEO On The Only Two Jobs Left In The Company Of The Future

In a wide-ranging conversation at Y Combinator, Replit CEO and co-founder Amjad Masad shared his vision for the future of software creation, the evolution of Replit's 'vibe coding' philosophy, and how AI is fundamentally changing the structure of the modern company.

The Mission: A Billion Developers

Replit’s mission has evolved from solving the headache of development environments to a much bolder goal: making programming accessible to anyone who can read and write. Masad envisions a world where a billion people can become developers, not by learning the 'painful' technical configurations traditional engineers often enjoy, but by using AI to bridge the gap between an idea and a deployed, scalable application.

The Rise of Vibe Coding

In late 2024, Replit introduced 'vibe coding,' a paradigm shift where users interface with AI using natural language and visual tools. With the release of Agent 4, this has expanded into a multi-agent architecture. Users can now utilize a visual canvas to design interfaces while agents work in the background to build features, handle merge conflicts, and even deploy apps directly to mobile platforms.

The Company of the Future

Masad posits a provocative future for the corporate world. As AI agents take over more specialized tasks, he believes companies will eventually be composed primarily of two roles:

Builders/Founders: Generalist entrepreneurs who identify business problems and 'deputize' agents to solve them.
Sales/Evangelists: Humans who focus on education, transformation, and building trust with other human clients.

At Replit, this is already being tested with 'vibe coding in residence' teams who operate with vague missions to find and automate inefficiencies across support and HR using their own tools.

Lessons from Y Combinator

Reflecting on his time in YC, Masad highlighted the program's lasting impact on Replit’s DNA. The philosophy of 'intense focus'—achieving more in three months than most do in years—and the drive for 7% week-over-week growth remain the bedrock of how Replit launches new initiatives today.

Looking Ahead: Post-Prompting

We are moving toward a 'post-prompting' world. Instead of specific, technical prompts, users will give agents high-level business goals, such as 'build me a SaaS company and market it.' In this landscape, the most valuable skills will be idea generation, a playful mindset toward new tools, and the persistence to keep trying as AI capabilities undergo massive step changes every six months.
