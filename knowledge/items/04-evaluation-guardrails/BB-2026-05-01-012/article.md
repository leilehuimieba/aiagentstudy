Perplexity’s recommendation for an Accessible Perceptual Uniform Triad based on its Internet explorations of color theory and empirical practice.

In this writing, I explore the capabilities of Perplexity to produce a perceptual uniform triad that passes color deficiency. Previously, I shared my similar adventures with the Claude, ChatGPT, DeepSeek, Copilot , Grok, and Gemini Generative AI chatbots. Rather than calculate a triad color scheme recommendation, Perplexity provides suggestions based on its search capabilities of color theory and empirical practice. The results are different from those I noted with other chatbots.

As highlighted in my earlier writings, a color space is perceptually uniform if a change of length in any direction X of the color space is perceived by a human as the same change. Non-uniform perceptual color spaces like RGB — Red Green Blue and RYB — Red Yellow Blue can have stark contrasts when transitioning from one hue to another hue.

In data visualization, these contrasts can be mistaken as changes in the data rather than as transitions in the color palette. As a result, many data visualization practitioners prefer to work in the perceptual uniform CIE LAB or Hue Chroma Luminance (HCL) color spaces.
