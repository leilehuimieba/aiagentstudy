## Core Takeaway

This in-depth investigation by The Athletic examines the financial dynamics between FIFA and the 11 U.S. host cities for the 2026 men's World Cup. It details how FIFA's hosting agreements require cities to cover massive costs—including transportation, security, stadium upgrades, and fan fest operations—while FIFA retains the vast majority of revenue from ticketing, media rights, and sponsorship. The article highlights specific examples of these costs, such as a $25.8 million jail in Kansas City, $57.8 million in waived tax revenue, and a $15 million broadcast center in Dallas that will be demolished. It also reveals a letter signed by 21 U.S. cities, including 7 of the 2026 host cities, warning U.S. Soccer that they will not accept similar terms for the 2031 Women's World Cup without meaningful negotiation on cost-sharing, revenue participation, and expense caps. The piece captures the growing exasperation of city officials, from the infamous 'put your city on the map' comment to the scaled-back fan fest plans and the push for a more equitable partnership in the future.
