## Core Takeaway

This article reviews a study by Winberg et al. that systematically evaluates ChatGPT-4.0 Pro's coding capabilities for quantitative research, specifically for causal inference methods like Difference-in-Differences, Inverse Probability Treatment Weighting, and Regression Discontinuity. The study uses benchmark solutions from 'Causal Inference: The Mixtape' and assesses performance across Python, R, and Stata based on accuracy, efficiency, errors, editing needs, and consistency. Results show ChatGPT performs better in Python and R than in Stata. The author then shares personal reflections on how LLMs have transformed their own workflow, including a strategic shift from SAS and RStudio to Python and VS Code, and the acceleration of data collection and analysis tasks. The article concludes that while AI is a powerful accelerator, human supervision, validation, and expertise remain essential, and that the tool's rise will create new dynamics in knowledge production and inequality.
