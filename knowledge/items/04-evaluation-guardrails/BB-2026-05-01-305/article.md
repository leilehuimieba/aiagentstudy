## Abstract

WebArena introduces a realistic and reproducible web environment with fully functional websites in e-commerce, forums, software development, and content management. It emphasizes benchmark tasks evaluated by functional correctness rather than text similarity.

## Key Evidence

- The environment is self-hostable and reproducible.
- The benchmark includes diverse, long-horizon tasks that emulate real human internet activity.
- The best GPT-4-based agent reaches only 14.41% end-to-end task success, versus 78.24% for humans.
