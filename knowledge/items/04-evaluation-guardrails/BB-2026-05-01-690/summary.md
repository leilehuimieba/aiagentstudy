## Core Takeaway

This article is part of The Athletic's World Cup performance series, written by performance expert Alan McCall. It focuses on the challenges of travel and accumulated fatigue as teams enter the knockout rounds. Drawing on interviews with players (Bacary Sagna, Ivan Rakitić, Per Mertesacker) and recovery specialists (Shona Halson, Darcy Norman, Anna West), the piece explains how jet lag, repeated travel, and disrupted sleep affect performance. It distinguishes between physical recovery (energy stores, muscle soreness) and mental fatigue, which is harder to measure and recover from. The author argues that preserving freshness requires not just logistics but also a supportive environment—trust, relationships, and communication. Examples include England's decision to return to a fixed base in Kansas City and Croatia's team atmosphere in 2018. The article concludes that the best plans cannot predict everything; human connection and adaptability are essential.
