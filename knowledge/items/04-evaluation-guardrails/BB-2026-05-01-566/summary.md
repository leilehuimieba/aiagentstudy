## Core Takeaway

This article is a complete compilation of Jensen Huang's interview with Condoleezza Rice at the Stanford Hoover Institution. The interview begins with Huang's experience immigrating from Thailand to the US at age 9 with his brother, attending a boarding school in Kentucky, and traces his academic and entrepreneurial journey at Oregon State University and Stanford. The core narrative revolves around NVIDIA's founding philosophy—challenging the dominance of general-purpose computing and betting on parallel computing architecture—and the subsequent decade-long 'hardship' period when GPU general-purpose computing was largely ignored before the deep learning explosion. Huang compares the AI revolution to a 'layer cake,' emphasizing that while energy, chips, and models form the foundation, the key to national competitiveness lies in application layers like healthcare, defense, and manufacturing. The interview concludes with a personal and national reflection, asserting that NVIDIA's success could only happen in America because it offers 'tailwinds of freedom' and trustworthy rules, stating bluntly, 'I am the personification of the American Dream.'
