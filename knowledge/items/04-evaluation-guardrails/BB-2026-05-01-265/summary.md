## Core Takeaway

This article is an in-depth conversation between Founder Park and Lucius founder Zhao He. Lucius is a company focused on enterprise-grade AI employees, but its core is not providing general AI capabilities; rather, it builds an organizational orchestration system called the 'Context Layer.' Zhao He argues that the biggest difference between AI and human employees is that the former must deliver deterministic results, thus requiring a clear 'employment contract' to define responsibilities, permissions, and SLAs. The article elaborates on Lucius's technical architecture, including an independent knowledge engine, state machine, and scheduler, which together enable the proactive collection, cleaning, and reuse of organizational context. Zhao He also shares his thoughts on the future form of AI employees, suggesting that organizations will eventually evolve into a 'pilot' model, where humans shift from executors to supervisors. He also candidly discusses the challenges faced by the team, such as engineering difficulty, market education costs, and team mindset management.
