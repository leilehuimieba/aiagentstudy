## Key Evidence

AgentForesight reframes failure handling as online auditing: instead of waiting until a trajectory ends badly, it tries to detect risky failure patterns as the trajectory unfolds. This is especially relevant for long-horizon agents that waste budget before they finally fail.

## Practical Notes

The main transfer is early-warning instrumentation for repetitive actions, contradictory evidence, or trajectory drift.
