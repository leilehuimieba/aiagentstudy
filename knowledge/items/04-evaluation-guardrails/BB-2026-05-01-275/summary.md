## Core Takeaway

This article is compiled from an in-depth podcast interview with Bjarne Stroustrup. The core discussion revolves around the limitations of AI programming: Stroustrup points out that code generated by LLMs is bloated, filled with old bugs and security vulnerabilities, and difficult to verify, leading senior developers to choose retirement rather than dealing with the verification work. He also systematically reviews the evolution of C++ over more than four decades, covering language design philosophy (emphasizing type-oriented programming and zero-overhead abstraction), the standardization process (committee consensus mechanism, shuttle diplomacy with IBM/Intel), the memory safety debate (over 90% of vulnerabilities originate from C-style code rather than C++ itself), and advice for beginners. The article also includes vivid narratives about the legendary culture of Bell Labs, interactions with Dennis Ritchie, and the cautionary tale of the Vasa warship.
