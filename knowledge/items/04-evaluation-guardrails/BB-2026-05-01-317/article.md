## Key Evidence

OWASP Benchmark packages ground truth in machine-readable form so that tool output can be compared against expected outcomes. This pattern is highly relevant whenever an agent system needs deterministic verification rather than narrative judgment.

## Practical Notes

For FlagHunter, the key transferable idea is not the domain (SAST) but the explicit expected-result schema.
