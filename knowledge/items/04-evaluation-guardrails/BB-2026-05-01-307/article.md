## Abstract

τ-bench evaluates agents that must interact with users, follow policy rules, and use domain-specific API tools. The benchmark uses retail and airline domains and scores agents by comparing the final database state against annotated goal states.

## Key Evidence

- The paper proposes pass^k as a reliability metric across multiple trials.
- GPT-4o succeeds on less than 50% of tasks.
- In retail, pass^8 is under 25%, highlighting large inconsistency.
