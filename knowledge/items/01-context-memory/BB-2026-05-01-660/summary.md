## Core Takeaway

This article is a transcript of an exclusive interview with Li Xunlei, Chief Economist of Zhongtai International, conducted by Jiemian News. From the perspective of M2, he systematically reviews the evolution of China's money creation mechanism: from the introduction of foreign capital in the early reform and opening-up period, to the export orientation after joining the WTO, and then to the credit-driven model of real estate plus infrastructure after 2008. After the long-term peak of the real estate cycle in 2021, M2 growth did not decline as expected, but instead remained high due to the rebound of foreign exchange reserves, the expansion of government bonds, and the increased share of direct financing. Li believes that the current economic driving forces have shifted to a dual-engine model of government counter-cyclical leverage and industrial upgrading. AI infrastructure, people's livelihood security, and other areas require huge fiscal expenditure, and direct financing will become the main channel for M2 expansion. He also discusses issues such as high real interest rates, the necessity of interest rate cuts, the space for government leverage, and the trade-off between efficiency and growth, emphasizing that GDP is an outcome indicator and the fundamental goal is to increase household income and consumption.
