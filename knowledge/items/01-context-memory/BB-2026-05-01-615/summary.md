## Core Takeaway

This article is compiled from an in-depth interview with Fiona Fung on Lenny's Podcast. Fiona is the engineering lead for Claude Code and Cowork, with over 25 years of experience in leadership roles at companies including IBM, Microsoft (working on Visual Studio and TypeScript), and Meta (founding Facebook Marketplace, working on AR glasses). The article revolves around the core insight that "writing code is exiting the bottleneck position." Key themes discussed include: When designers and PMs can all submit code, validation replaces coding as the new bottleneck; teams should codify what "good" means into specifications stored in the repository, and let Claude verify against them; hiring is limited to two types—builders with product dreams and deep system experts who tackle tough problems, matched with the principle of "trust but verify"; management is being reshaped by asynchronous culture—a permanent Claude remote session is attached to all codebases, maintaining full awareness at all times. Routines go beyond cron jobs by having a "brain" that can autonomously generate prompts, dispatch agents, and produce reviewable PRs; high agency must be paired with high accountability—grant freedom while requiring clear validation hypotheses and responsible parties; managers must live in the product and practice dogfooding—when data and individual cases conflict, sometimes trust the case; the differentiator is a growth mindset, and what holds people back is fear—encourage doing things that scare you. The article also covers JIT (Just-in-Time) planning, role fusion for engineers, proactively managing quality with a "bad & sad" framework, and the culture of cutting useless processes. The compilation retains the essence of the original interview with added editor commentary, offering high value for engineering managers in the AI era.
