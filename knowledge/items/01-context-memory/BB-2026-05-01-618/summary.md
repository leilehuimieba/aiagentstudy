## Core Takeaway

This article is a compilation of a speech by Ma Zhen, head of the basic platform at Sina Weibo's R&D center, at a technology conference. It provides a detailed introduction to the overall approach and implementation cases of AI Agent collaborative operations at Weibo. It first analyzes the escalating complexity of operations brought by cloud-native and multi-cloud environments, as well as the limitations of traditional AIOps such as data silos, loss of experience, and high human-machine interaction barriers. It then proposes a three-step strategy: AI-assisted programming, foundational capability building (operations knowledge base and MCP tool encapsulation), and AI Agent construction. It highlights three representative scenarios — Hot Event Response AI Agent, Root Cause Analysis Public Opinion Agent, and Client Crash Analysis — each outlining the pain points of traditional methods and the significant improvements brought by AI solutions. Finally, it summarizes insights from the implementation: not all scenarios require large models, importance of data cleaning and cost control, human-machine collaboration to address hallucinations, and assistance from smaller models. Overall, it presents a capability layering of AI + AIOps (AI-assisted development, foundational capability base, and AI Agent-assisted decision-making), offering a reference path for operations teams of similar scale.
