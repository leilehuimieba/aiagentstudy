## Core Takeaway

This Guardian feature tells the story of Emily, whose father Mark was arrested after an undercover police officer discovered him describing detailed fantasies of sexually abusing her in online chat rooms. Despite Emily’s insistence that the abuse never happened, the police pursued charges for indecent communication. However, the Crown Prosecution Service dropped the case, arguing that because Mark’s messages were shared privately with an apparently like-minded recipient, they could not prove the material was intended to be indecent or obscene. The article follows Emily and her mother Fiona through the shock of the arrest, the unraveling of their family, and Emily’s subsequent determination to challenge the legal gap. It includes expert commentary from law professor Clare McGlynn and child abuse expert Michael Sheath, who argue that such fantasy chat normalizes and escalates interest in child abuse. Emily’s advocacy—meeting with her MP and the minister for victims—has contributed to recent legislative amendments banning step-incest pornography and performer role-playing as children, but a specific offence for text-based advocacy of child sexual abuse remains absent.
