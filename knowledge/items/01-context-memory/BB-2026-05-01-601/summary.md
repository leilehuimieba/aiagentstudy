## Core Takeaway

In response to a reader's question about avoiding authoritarian leadership, Charity offers a nuanced perspective on engineering management. She argues that the industry cultivates authoritarianism by venerating winners and retconning their myths. The core of leadership is winning at business—understanding strategy, context, and goals. Many managers enter the role wanting to protect their teams from bad experiences, but they often misdiagnose problems like lack of product-market fit or misalignment as micromanagement. She advocates that humane, empathetic leaders can build better businesses long-term, but they must also be skilled operators. For advancement to director, she advises focusing on business learning and being honest about team performance rather than cheerleading. She concludes that managers must earn their roles by setting high standards and running the business efficiently, citing Twitter's collapse as a cautionary tale.
