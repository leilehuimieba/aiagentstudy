## Core Takeaway

The article begins with a suspended 1 billion yuan consumer fund project from a state enterprise investment manager, providing detailed coverage of how, after the June 2026 State Council "Guiding Opinions" and SASAC "Measures" took effect, central and state enterprises and local state asset systems simultaneously launched special rectification of private equity funds. Through interviews with multiple state enterprise investment managers, risk control heads, law firm partners, and local state asset platform leaders, the article presents the real picture of comprehensive suspension of fund approvals, close examination of existing fund ledgers, and various localities' property rights exchanges concentrating on listing and clearing non-core and inefficient fund assets. The author reviews the regulatory blind spots accumulated during the previous rapid expansion of state-owned funds—namely the disconnect between state asset investment supervision and private equity industry supervision systems, as well as the problems of funds established "for the sake of establishing," divorced from core businesses, and proliferation of financial-type funds. The article also discusses the internal debates on the boundaries of "non-core business" during rectification, the transformation of fund positioning from "scale-first, financial-oriented" to "industry-priority, patient capital," and the challenges of investment team capability transformation, fundraising renegotiations, and LP discussions. Ultimately, through interviewees' statements, it concludes: this is not contraction, but a return to proper positioning.
