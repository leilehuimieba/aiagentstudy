## Core Takeaway

This article, from The New Yorker's Annals of Religion, offers an in-depth look at the rise of Club America, the high-school offshoot of Turning Point USA, following the assassination of its founder, Charlie Kirk. Through intimate portraits of teenage followers in Palm Beach County—including Gracie Bell Joyce, a sixteen-year-old unschooler who started a local chapter—and students at the elite Benjamin School, the reporter examines how Kirk's fusion of evangelical Christianity and far-right politics has galvanized a generation. The piece also captures the tensions within schools and among peers, as liberal students like Liam Duffy push back against Kirk's rhetoric on race, gender, and immigration. By weaving together personal stories, historical context on the Seven Mountains Mandate, and the impact of the COVID-19 pandemic on teens' distrust of authority, the article provides a nuanced exploration of the current youth religious-right movement in America.
