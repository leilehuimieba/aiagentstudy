## Core Takeaway

Based on an analysis of over $700,000 in LinkedIn Ads spend (63,000+ clicks, 8.1 million impressions) and corresponding Google Ads data from the same B2B SaaS and professional services clients, the article delivers granular CPC benchmarks. It finds that while LinkedIn's blended average CPC ($11.12) is roughly double Google's ($5.45), the gap narrows significantly when comparing high-intent, cold audiences: non-branded Google Search averages $12.48 versus $13.94 for LinkedIn prospecting campaigns. The author breaks down LinkedIn CPCs by ad objective (ranging from $4.45 for engagement to $71.43 for video views) and by industry (B2B SaaS ~$11.02, professional services ~$15.25), then provides a budget framework ($3k–$5k minimum viable, $5k–$10k testing, $10k+ scaling) and recommends using Google/Microsoft Ads for intent capture and LinkedIn for audience-led demand generation. The article concludes that LinkedIn's higher raw CPC is often justified by access to specific professional audiences and earlier-stage influence in B2B buying cycles.
