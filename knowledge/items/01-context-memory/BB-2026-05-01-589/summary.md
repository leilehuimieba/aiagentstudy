## Core Takeaway

The article details how the JD Health OPC (One Person Company) team adapted Anthropic's open-source 8 Product Management Skills (synthesize-research, metrics-review, competitive-brief, product-brainstorming, write-spec, roadmap-update, sprint-planning, stakeholder-update) to enhance their product workflow. The author notes that in an OPC team without dedicated product roles, this set of Skills provides a "shared product-engineering decision-making framework" that standardizes the process from requirement emergence to launch. The article unfolds across four stages: the first stage uses synthesize-research and metrics-review to determine if a problem is worth solving; the second stage uses product-brainstorming to diverge on solutions and write-spec to converge on requirement specifications; the third stage uses roadmap-update to determine priorities and sprint-planning to implement the iteration plan; the fourth stage uses metrics-review to review metrics and stakeholder-update to sync with different audiences. The article also provides a 5-field format for material input, prompt templates for each stage, a roadmap change description template, common pitfalls, and a minimal starting path.
