## Core Takeaway

Written by Austin Lau, a growth marketer at Anthropic, this article serves as a practical guide for non-technical knowledge workers to get started with Claude Cowork. It clearly delineates the use cases for Claude Chat (for questions, brainstorming, and quick answers) versus Claude Cowork (for multi-step tasks that produce a deliverable like a report, deck, or spreadsheet). The author introduces a five-ingredient checklist to identify tasks suitable for delegation to Claude Cowork: multiple inputs, a file output, recurrence, a known definition of 'good,' and a boring middle process. The article then details three real-world workflows from the author's own work: a daily briefing that summarizes Slack and email, a budget pacing dashboard for performance marketing, and an automated reporting workflow connected to Google Search Console. It concludes with a step-by-step guide for the first 10 minutes of use, emphasizing the importance of providing rich context and making Claude ask clarifying questions before starting a task.
