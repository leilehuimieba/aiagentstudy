## Core Takeaway

This article is a transcript of a podcast interview. The guests are Agan and Liu Kai, two partners of Rolling AI, an AI business consulting firm. Using the simultaneous announcement by OpenAI and Anthropic of a billion-dollar FDE joint venture as a starting point, the article delves into the nature, value, and challenges of the emerging FDE role. The core argument is that AI is not traditional software, but labor. The FDE acts like a 'foreman,' leading a team of 'digital high-performers' deep into the front lines of a business to solve three major problems: business process integration, private knowledge governance, and IT system connectivity. Through multiple real-world cases involving a dairy company, a retail chain, a rental platform, and others, the article demonstrates how AI is moving from 'demonstrating capability' to 'delivering results.' It presents sharp viewpoints such as 'SOPs represent backwardness' and 'AI implementation failure rate exceeds 50%.' The article also discusses the core qualities required for an FDE (strategic consulting, human-machine collaboration mindset, engineering hands-on skills), the changes in organizational structure in the AI era (from control center to empowerment center), and the future business prospect where FDE service providers might be 'owned' rather than 'invested in' by PE giants.
