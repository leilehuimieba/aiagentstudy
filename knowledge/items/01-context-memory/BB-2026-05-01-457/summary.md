## Core Takeaway

This article is a transcript of an in-depth conversation between Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella and LinkedIn co-founder Reid Hoffman. Starting with his personal passion for poetry, Nadella draws an analogy between large AI models and the "compression" of knowledge, and introduces a new foundational concept for the AI era: the "Climbing Machine," which uses data and reinforcement learning to continuously approach a target. He further elaborates on "Token Capital" as a new economic engine alongside human capital, warning businesses to protect their "tacit knowledge" from being leaked by AI models. Regarding the future of work, Nadella sees development evolving from code completion to autonomous agent loops, proposing the concept of an "Agent Development Environment." He also details Microsoft's AI governance plan, "Agent 365," emphasizing observability and auditing. On a macro level, Nadella argues that national sovereignty should manifest as enabling domestic enterprises to thrive in the Token economy, rather than disconnecting from the technological frontier. He calls on the tech industry to rebuild societal trust through tangible actions, proving AI is a positive-sum game, and dreams of humanity overcoming its bounded rationality to achieve a 10% compound annual growth rate for global GDP.
