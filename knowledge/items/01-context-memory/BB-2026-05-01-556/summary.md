## Core Takeaway

This collection of fragments from Martin Fowler covers several key themes in the current tech landscape. It begins with 'Prag Dave' Thomas's reflection on how LLMs have rekindled his love for programming by removing drudgery and speeding up feedback loops. Fowler then shares insights from the DDD Europe conference, highlighting Eric Evans's experiments with LLMs and Chelsea Troy's framework for managing LLM context windows through four distinct conversation registers: Exploring, Brainstorming, Deciding, and Implementing. Charity Majors warns of a dangerous crevasse forming between AI enthusiasts and skeptics, arguing that both sides have valid points and that the path forward requires honest storytelling and treating AI as an engineering problem. Simon Willison observes that Anthropic and OpenAI have found product-market fit with coding agents, leading to aggressive enterprise pricing. Finally, Mike Masnick analyzes the internet's failed promise of decentralization, explaining how new middlemen replaced old gatekeepers, leading to enshittification, and argues that user-controlled data and ease of exit are the keys to fighting digital despotism.
