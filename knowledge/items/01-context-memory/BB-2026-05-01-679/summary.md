## Core Takeaway

Drawing on two studies conducted a decade apart, the authors compare how technology founders approach early sales. The latest research, based on semi-structured interviews with 250+ founders across 30+ countries, reveals persistent mistakes (overestimating product-market fit, pursuing overly broad markets, lacking sales background) and new challenges specific to today's hypercompetitive landscape: mistaking attention for traction, facing overwhelming competition where differentiation is no longer enough, and struggling to create urgency. The article then introduces the SPRINT framework (Speed, Problem, Results, Implementation, Niche, Trust) as a practical approach for founder-led sales to cut through noise and convert curiosity into commitment. A case study of a German founder applying SPRINT illustrates the transformation. The framework helps founders diagnose where deals stall and establish what prospects need before committing.
