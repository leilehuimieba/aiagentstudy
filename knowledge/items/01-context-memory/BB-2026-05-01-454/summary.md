## Core Takeaway

This article by Sam Altman and Jakub Pachocki announces OpenAI's entry into its third phase, shifting from research and product deployment to making advanced AI abundant, affordable, safe, and useful for everyone. It draws a historical parallel to the electrification of rural America to argue that transformative technologies deliver their deepest impact when access is widespread. The authors commit to building AI in service of humanity, emphasizing that power should be broadly distributed rather than concentrated among a few companies or governments. They outline three main goals: building an automated AI researcher (with an internal belief that by March 2028 a significant fraction of research will be done by AI systems), accelerating the economy, and giving every person on Earth a personal AGI. The piece also addresses risks, calling for international coordination on safety standards and arguing that human judgment, values, and public oversight become more important as AI capabilities advance. It explicitly rejects a future of total automation, stating that AI should augment human agency, not replace it.
