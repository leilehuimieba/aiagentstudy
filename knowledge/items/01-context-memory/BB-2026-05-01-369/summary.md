## Core Takeaway

This article is a long-form interview with RAGFlow founder Zhang Yingfeng, part of CSDN's "Gravity" series. It begins with Zhang's personal journey, recounting his transition from a veteran in search technology to his first entrepreneurial venture, and details InfiniFlow's strategic pivot from developing the AI-native database Infinity to making RAGFlow its flagship product. The core discussion revolves around the present and future of RAG technology: Zhang firmly believes that "RAG is dead" is marketing rhetoric, asserting that retrieval itself is eternal. Especially in the age of agents, RAG users are shifting from humans to agents, fundamentally changing retrieval frequency, query length, and product form. The article also delves into the challenges of open-source commercialization, including long payment cycles and service-based payment models in the domestic market, as well as the strategic necessity of going global. It covers the technical decision to rewrite the online retrieval component of RAGFlow 1.0 in Go to boost performance. Additionally, Zhang shares the gains and losses of being a "veteran" entrepreneur, emphasizing adherence to core principles and a steadfast belief in long-term value.
