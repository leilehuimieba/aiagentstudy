## Core Takeaway

This ProPublica and Drilled investigation details how BP's $15 million donation to Princeton University's Carbon Mitigation Initiative led to the creation of the paradigm-setting 'Wedges' climate paper. The paper made climate change seem solvable without abandoning fossil fuels, heavily promoting carbon capture and storage. BP executives reviewed multiple drafts, suggested language, and even attempted a rewrite. The paper's overselling of carbon capture technology, which remains unproven at scale, has influenced U.S. policy and international climate reports for two decades. The investigation reveals a coordinated strategy by oil companies to shape academic research, steer public perception, and delay a transition away from fossil fuels by funding research aligned with their interests.
