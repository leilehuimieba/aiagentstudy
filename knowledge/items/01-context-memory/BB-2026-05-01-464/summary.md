## Core Takeaway

Based on the practices of Baidu's e-commerce search team, this article systematically explains how to systematically improve code quality in the era of AI-Coding. The core framework includes: Harness engineering as the infrastructure, full-stack capability as the organizational model, and three-layer constraints (input/generation/output) as the quality control mechanism. Through three progressive project cases (Brand Card iteration, List Automation, Outfit Assistant Agent), it demonstrates the application effects of this method across projects of varying complexity, including development cycle compression, testing efficiency improvement, and faster anomaly detection. The article also details the transformation of the RD and QA collaboration model, upgrading from linear handoff to closed-loop co-creation, with QA shifting left to the standard-setting phase and RD outputting standardized Specs, jointly achieving testing efficiency gains and delivery quality assurance. Finally, it outlines the expansion plans for the team and business levels going forward.
