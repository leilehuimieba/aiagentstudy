## Core Takeaway

This is the full text of the 2026 commencement address delivered by Cheng Lesong, head of the Philosophy Department at Peking University. The speech opens with a self-deprecating take on the 'involution' of graduation speeches and the anxiety of 'preachy chicken soup,' before pivoting to a serious exploration of the nature of philosophy. Cheng Lesong argues that philosophy serves as a quality inspector of thought and a cleanser of ideas, and that its core lies in subjecting the very questions that trouble our minds to careful and critical scrutiny. He criticizes the contemporary tendency toward self-deception through faulty ways of questioning — such as submitting emotional matters to rational judgment — and emphasizes that reflective awareness and critical capacity are the gifts philosophy offers. The article delves into modern symptoms such as the confusion between information overload and sensory overload, the hollowing out of value caused by the inflation of meaning, and the mental fragility born of excessive reflection. He pushes back against the posture of 'seeing through everything' and advocates for a measured balance between conviction and doubt. In closing, he addresses the graduates: life is a lush valley, a rough terrain — march forward resolutely amid the clamor of voices and live out a genuine argument.
