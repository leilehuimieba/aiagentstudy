## Core Takeaway

Aditya Kumarakrishnan, a Technical Fellow at Walmart Global Tech, presents a talk on building AI agents that can outlast the hype cycle. He identifies a pattern of 'catastrophic forgetting' where each AI peak and valley leads to relearning old lessons. The core of the talk is four foundational ideas often overlooked in current agent discourse. First, he advocates for a 'stronger notion of agents,' liberating the concept from being solely tied to LLMs and viewing it as a general-purpose, human-oriented computing abstraction. Second, he argues for building modular and extensible agents using a cognitive architecture (like CoALA), which allows for easy evolution from one agent architecture (e.g., ReAct) to another (e.g., CodeAct) without a full rewrite. Third, he emphasizes leveraging the mature field of process science, including workflow engines for durability and scalability, and process mining for discovering and understanding existing business processes. Finally, he introduces the concept of 'terraforming the environment' for agents, arguing that the most capable agent is limited by a hostile, non-ergonomic digital environment. He calls for reshaping systems to be more navigable, observable, and actionable for agents, moving beyond just adding an MCP interface.
