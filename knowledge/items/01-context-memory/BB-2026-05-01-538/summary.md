## Core Takeaway

Starting with the US Department of Commerce's export control ban on Anthropic's flagship models, Fable 5 and Mythos 5, on June 12, 2026, the article details the full scope of the event. The trigger was a security test report submitted to the government by Amazon, claiming the model could be jailbroken for cyberattacks. However, Anthropic and several independent security researchers argued that the jailbreak was 'narrow and non-general,' far from constituting a national security threat. The article further reveals the underlying political grievances: Anthropic's relationship with the Trump administration had been strained due to its refusal of Pentagon contract terms and being labeled a 'supply chain risk.' The deepest irony lies in the fact that Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei had previously written an article advocating for the government's right to prevent or withdraw the deployment of high-risk models. Yet, when the government actually exercised this power, it lacked the transparent procedures and checks and balances he had argued for. The article ultimately leads the discussion to the core dilemma of AI governance: who has the right to shut down the 'genius nation in the data center,' and by what rules should this power be exercised.
