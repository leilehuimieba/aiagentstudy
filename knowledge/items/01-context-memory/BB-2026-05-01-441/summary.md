## Core Takeaway

Authored by Shah Rahman, Global Head of Autonomous ML Iteration & Optimization for Ads at Meta, this piece cuts through the hype around AI-generated code to define what AI-native engineering actually requires. It argues that the real productivity gains come from engineers transitioning from writing code to orchestrating it. The article outlines four core practices: synchronized context engineering, specification-driven development, critical verification, and problem decomposition. It provides a detailed time allocation model (40% context-setting, 20% generation, 40% review) and a three-phase individual transformation journey (Foundation, Integration, Mastery). The piece also introduces the Agentic Development Life Cycle (ADLC) as a replacement for traditional SDLC, covers team cultural foundations, and dedicates a substantial section to security guardrails, including real incident examples and emerging controls. It concludes by emphasizing that domain expertise is the key differentiator and that this is a multi-year transformation, not a simple tool upgrade.
