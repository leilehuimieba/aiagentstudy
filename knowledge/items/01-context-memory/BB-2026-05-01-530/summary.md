## Core Takeaway

This deeply reported piece by Ben Taub traces the evolution of Donald Trump's obsession with acquiring Greenland from a 2018 dinner-table fantasy into a concerted, multi-pronged campaign during his second term. The article profiles four key figures—Chris Cox (Bikers for Trump founder), Tom Dans (former venture capitalist and Treasury official), Drew Horn (former Special Forces commander), and Jørgen Boassen (a Greenlandic supporter)—who ran private influence operations in Greenland, often without the knowledge of the Danish or Greenlandic governments. It details the secret National Security Council task force (the Greenland Policy Coordination Committee) formed during Trump's first term, which worked to accelerate Greenlandic independence in a way that would increase U.S. control, while simultaneously undermining Denmark. The story culminates in the post-2024 reality where Trump's threats have become official policy, Danish soldiers prepare to blow up runways to deter a U.S. invasion, and the Danish Prime Minister declares the post-WWII world order is over. The piece is a stark examination of how personal whims and informal channels have replaced traditional diplomacy, turning a former ally into a potential target.
