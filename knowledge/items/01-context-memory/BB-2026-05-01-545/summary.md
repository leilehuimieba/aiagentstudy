## Core Takeaway

This article from The Pragmatic Engineer newsletter reports on a nascent but significant trend: engineering departments are starting to question and cut back on rapidly growing AI spending, particularly on AI coding tools. It opens with Uber's president questioning the ROI of AI-driven code commits, noting the difficulty in linking metrics like '25% of code commits via AI' to actual shipped features. The article then gathers perspectives from various companies: OpenCode sees surging demand for cheaper model hosting; cutting-edge companies feel forced to pay an 'intelligence premium' but are exploring model routing; DoorDash gives devs responsibility for their token usage; a traditional company imposes monthly limits and downgrades to cheaper models; and startups are buying multiple subscriptions to avoid API costs. The piece concludes by noting a new bottom-up focus on AI efficiency, with engineers experimenting with cheaper models and routing, and predicts that saving on AI costs will become a rewarded metric in performance reviews.
