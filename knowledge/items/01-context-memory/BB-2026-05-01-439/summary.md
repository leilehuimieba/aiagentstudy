## Core Takeaway

In this interview, GitHub COO Kyle Daigle addresses the unprecedented challenges and opportunities AI coding agents present to the platform. He details how GitHub's infrastructure, originally designed for human-paced development, is straining under a 14x increase in commits and a 1400% growth in agent activity. Daigle explains GitHub's internal AI rollout strategy, which emphasizes integrating AI into existing workflows (Slack, Teams, GitHub) rather than forcing new tools. He introduces the concept of 'micro-skills'—small, atomic AI skills that do one thing well—over brittle 'mega-skills.' Daigle also shares his personal workflow of running 15 agents on Saturdays to synthesize company context from PRs, Slack, Teams transcripts, and Obsidian notes, enabling him to look backward before planning forward. The conversation covers the evolution of Copilot, the future of pull requests in an agent-dominated world, the challenges of open-source maintainers facing AI-generated 'slop,' and why GitHub refuses to hide the code even as it becomes a platform for low-code builders.
