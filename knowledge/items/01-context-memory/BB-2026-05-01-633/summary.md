## Core Takeaway

This article is an in-depth interview by InfoQ with Professor Qiu Xipeng, a Fudan University professor and founder of Mosi AI. As the publisher of China's first conversational large language model MOSS, Qiu systematically elaborates on the technical path of 'Context Intelligence' he proposed. The article first defines Context Intelligence as the inevitable direction for large model development, encompassing five stages: perception, representation, delivery, persistence, and feedback loop. It then details the architecture design and collaboration mechanisms of the MOSS series sub-models: MOSS-Audio (general audio understanding), MOSS-VL (video understanding), and MOSS-TTS (high-fidelity speech synthesis), emphasizing a unified representation centered on discrete tokens. On technical challenges, Qiu analyzes how to address computational pressure from context expansion from three levels: representation compression, model architecture, and inference system, and compares the essential differences between 'full-modal unified discrete modeling' and mainstream 'bridge-style' approaches. Addressing data bottlenecks, the article proposes constructing high-quality contextual data through synthesis pipelines and world models, pointing out that data processing pipelines are the core differentiation barrier among models. Finally, Qiu summarizes the practical references the MOSS series provides for the industry in three aspects: approach, methodology, and ecosystem, emphasizing the role of open-source strategies in driving ecosystem prosperity.
