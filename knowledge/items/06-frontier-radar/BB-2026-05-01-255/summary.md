## Core Takeaway

Tencent Hunyuan has officially open-sourced its next-generation translation model, Hy-MT2, available in three sizes: 1.8B, 7B, and 30B-A3B. All models support translation across 33 languages, as well as 5 minority languages and dialects. The model demonstrates excellent performance in general translation, specialized domain translation, and real-world business scenarios. The 7B and 30B-A3B models surpass open-source models like DeepSeek-V4-Pro in multiple evaluations, approaching the level of Gemini 3.1 Pro. Hy-MT2 introduces a Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architecture for the first time and achieves extreme 1.25-bit quantization based on the proprietary Sherry framework. The 1.8B model requires only 440MB of storage, enabling local inference on mobile phone chips with a 1.5x speed improvement over the previous generation. Concurrently, the 'Tencent Hy Translation' mini-program based on Hy-MT2 has been launched, supporting voice input, customizable translation styles, and offline translation.
