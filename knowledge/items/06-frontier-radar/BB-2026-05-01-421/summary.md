## Core Takeaway

This article is compiled from a speech by Dr. Peng Junran, Associate Professor at the University of Science and Technology Beijing, at QCon 2026 Beijing. Starting from the endgame judgment of artificial intelligence, the article argues that language intelligence will replace everything possible in the virtual space within five to ten years and will inevitably radiate into the physical space. The world model is key to closing the loop from cognition to physical execution. Since 2023, the team has been exploring three technical routes in parallel: large-scale scene reconstruction based on 3D Gaussian Splatting, optimized by introducing geometric priors and material properties to enable physical simulation capabilities; intelligent content generation based on graphics engines, using Agent-driven approaches to automatically generate complete scenes from language descriptions, applied in autonomous driving simulation and embodied intelligence services; and video generation-assisted reconstruction, addressing reconstruction degradation in sparse views through iterative refinement. Ultimately, the team proposed a 4D world model system, where a feed-forward 4D generation model and a spatial refinement model iterate with each other to generate free-viewpoint dynamic volumetric videos from 2D videos, fundamentally eliminating spatiotemporal inconsistencies. The article points out that an end-to-end 4D generation model is the ideal ultimate form.
