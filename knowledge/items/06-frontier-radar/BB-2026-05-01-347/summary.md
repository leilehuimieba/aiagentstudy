## Core Takeaway

The article presents a data-driven analysis of how AI agents are transforming B2B procurement, citing the 2X AI Innovation Lab's AI Visibility Index which found 96% of 70 analyzed B2B companies were functionally invisible in agent-driven discovery. It argues that traditional marketing infrastructure—built for human readers who click links and read pages—produces signals that only humans can read, while AI agents parse structured data, query APIs, and return synthesized recommendations. The article identifies three critical infrastructure gaps: machine-readable market presence (OpenAPI specs, schema.org markup), product instrumentation for non-human callers (APIs over UIs), and converting brand proof into structured data (from PDFs to queryable records). It uses data from Deloitte, BCG, Salesforce, and ServiceNow to demonstrate the scale and urgency of this shift, concluding that closing the visibility gap requires an engineering solution, not a marketing campaign.
