## Core Takeaway

This article announces a historic breakthrough: the complete virtual unwrapping and reading of PHerc. 1667, a carbonized scroll from Herculaneum sealed since 79 AD. The scroll was scanned with high-resolution phase-contrast X-ray microtomography at the European Synchrotron Radiation Facility, then digitally flattened and read using machine learning to detect faint ink. The recovered text is a Stoic philosophical treatise, possibly by Chrysippus or his school. The same method also confirmed the reading of another scroll (PHerc. Paris 4) with higher-resolution imaging and identified the title and author of PHerc. 139 (Philodemus, On Gods, Book 8). All data, code, and transcriptions are openly released, and the work was achieved via the open Vesuvius Challenge community.
