## Core Takeaway

This analysis by three economists examines the 2026 FIFA World Cup group-stage ticket resale market (StubHub) to uncover what drives fan demand. By isolating team and city effects from the lowest single-ticket prices, the authors find that host nations (Mexico, US, Canada) command huge premiums, as do teams featuring Lionel Messi (Argentina) and Cristiano Ronaldo (Portugal). Brazil retains strong appeal. In contrast, traditional European powers (England, Germany, France, Netherlands) have negative estimated team effects, likely because European fans face high costs and stricter border policies. City effects show Miami and Mexican host cities dominate, while West Coast venues underperform. The authors also note that the data reflects pre-tournament expectations and has limitations, but provides a rare window into North American soccer fandom shaped by patriotism, immigration, and geography.
