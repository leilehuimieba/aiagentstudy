## Core Takeaway

This article is a distilled record of a closed-door Meetup themed 'Make for Agent' by Ma Duoling, a senior researcher at Geek Park. It points out that the real pain points for current Agent entrepreneurs are not about distant technological prospects, but fundamental issues like identity, permissions, context, sense of control, and accountability attribution. The discussion revolves around several micro-divergences: whether Agents need IM, whether humans should intervene in goals, processes, or results, whether Agents should have persistent identities, and whether enterprise customers buy efficiency or control. The article extracts seven core product insights: limited identity, responsibility-based communication, prioritizing responsibility containers, intervening at critical breakpoints, context infrastructure, positioning in the accountability chain, and dual-sided design. The final conclusion is that the core of Make for Agent is not designing interfaces for AI, but redesigning the accountability chain within products. When Agents become execution units, goals, permissions, context, actions, and consequences are systematically decoupled for the first time, and products can no longer avoid designing the accountability chain.
