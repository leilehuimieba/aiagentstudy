## Core Takeaway

This in-depth report, based on interviews with multiple Afghan women and their families, details the devastating impact of the Taliban's April 2026 decree on judicial separation. The decree outlines 12 grounds for divorce, but each requires the husband's consent or payment of an unlimited sum (khul). The law also explicitly allows child marriage at any age. Through the harrowing stories of Fatima, Ruqya, Habiba, Mina, and others, the article shows how women who attempt to leave abusive or forced marriages are met with armed force, impossible financial demands, and a legal system that systematically prioritises male authority. Even before the decree, women faced a difficult path; now it is virtually closed. The report includes testimonies from judges, families, and women in hiding, painting a stark picture of institutionalised gender oppression.
