## Core Takeaway

The article uses the January 2026 US software stock crash (SaaSpocalypse) as a starting point, pointing out that AI Agents are fundamentally shaking the SaaS business model. Core arguments include: 1) AI bypasses traditional software interfaces via APIs, eroding the SaaS middle layer (e.g., Thomson Reuters, Atlassian) whose moat was built on interfaces and usage habits, while underlying systems involving compliance and auditing (e.g., ERP) remain relatively stable due to high switching costs; 2) The total amount of software will not decrease; instead, the exponential drop in token costs (Jevons Paradox) will spawn more new software, with software transforming from a fixed product to an on-demand generated medium; 3) The Agent era requires rebuilding three layers of infrastructure: protocols (MCP), payments (x402), and distribution (browse.sh); 4) Entry-level engineering positions are being squeezed by AI, but value is shifting from writing code to understanding systems, giving rise to the new role of FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer). The article comprehensively cites viewpoints from a16z, Benedict Evans, Noah Smith, and others, with detailed data and rigorous logic.
