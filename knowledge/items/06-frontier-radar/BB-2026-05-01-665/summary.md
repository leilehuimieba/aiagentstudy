## Core Takeaway

Based on a recent meta-analysis on leisure exercise and longevity, the author builds an interactive calculator to estimate how much exercise is optimal for an individual, factoring in not only the health benefits (~3.5 extra years of life) but also how much one enjoys or dislikes exercise. Key findings: benefits plateau at roughly 5-12 hours per week; those who hate exercise should still do a few hours weekly, with higher intensity offering more benefit per minute; finding a sport you love yields a huge improvement in quality-adjusted life years (~2.5 life-year equivalents). The article includes caveats about observational bias and age effects, providing a nuanced, rational framework for exercise decisions.
