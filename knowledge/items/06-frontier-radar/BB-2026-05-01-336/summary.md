## Core Takeaway

This article is compiled from a speech by Huang Chao, an Assistant Professor at the University of Hong Kong, at the 2026 China AIGC Industry Summit, systematically elaborating his deep thoughts and practices on the AI Agent ecosystem. The core viewpoint is: The Agent era requires redesigning the infrastructure of the digital world, rather than forcing Agents to laboriously adapt to human interfaces. Huang Chao's team open-sourced the lightweight general-purpose Agent nanobot, emphasizing the design philosophy of 'simplicity is the ultimate sophistication.' They proposed the CLI-Anything solution, arguing that CLI is more suitable for Agent Computer Use than GUI. By wrapping professional software into command-line interfaces, Agents can directly drive complex applications like 3D modeling and design tools. Regarding self-evolution, the team chose a skill-based route, achieving skill accumulation through Open Space. Experiments showed a significant reduction in token consumption and improved task completion rates. They also used 8 Agents to coordinate 8 H100 GPUs for distributed model training, verifying the feasibility of Agent Swarm, but found that 3-5 Agents might be the optimal scale, with diminishing marginal returns beyond that. The article also discusses core challenges such as long-horizon task robustness, Agents learning from mistakes, and persistent environment alignment.
