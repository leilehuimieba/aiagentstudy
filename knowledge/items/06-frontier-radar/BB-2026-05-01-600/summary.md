## Core Takeaway

In an exclusive interview at APOS, Disney+ Asia Pacific chief Tony Zameczkowski discusses the platform's regional strategy. He details the success of Korean content like 'Perfect Crown' and 'Battle of Fates', the soft launch of ESPN in select markets with localized esports rights (KeSPA in Korea), the potential for vertical short-form discovery (Verts), and the gradual rollout of the ad-supported tier. He emphasizes sustainable growth over cost, the importance of localization in payment and content, and the unique advantage of Disney's ecosystem (parks, cruise, Hulu, ESPN). On Japan, he sees immense creativity and plans more live-action productions, hoping they will eventually travel like Korean content. The interview also touches on the 'one Disney' vision and the need for focus amid competition.
