## Core Takeaway

Using the release of DeepSeek V4 as a catalyst, the article points out that China's domestic computing ecosystem is undergoing a fundamental transformation: from a one-way model where chips passively adapt to models, to a new 'chip-model synergy' stage where models, frameworks, and chips evolve collaboratively. The core material comes from the developer roundtable at the Kunpeng Ascend Developer Conference 2026. Through real-world cases from four teams—AIGCode, Tsinghua University, USTC, and a leading joint-stock bank—the article demonstrates substantial progress in the Kunpeng Ascend ecosystem from 'usable' to 'excellent' across three dimensions: adaptation efficiency, performance ceiling, and production-grade trust. Specific data includes: AIGCode achieving 65% MFU for MoE model pre-training on Ascend (nearly double the industry average), USTC achieving an average 40x speedup with its LU solver based on Kunpeng, and a bank integrating AI into its core risk control process, achieving financial-grade metrics such as 500ms first-token latency, 26 billion daily tokens, and 99.999% availability. The article emphasizes that the open-sourcing of CANN is a critical turning point for the ecosystem, where developers have shifted from 'submitting requirements and waiting for adaptation' to 'solving problems themselves and actively contributing code,' leading to self-sustaining growth.
