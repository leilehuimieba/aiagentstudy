## Core Takeaway

Taking Lagou's voluntary bankruptcy application in 2026 as the starting point, this article systematically reviews the complete lifecycle of this vertical recruitment platform from birth to demise. The author closely ties Lagou's fate to the rise and fall of China's mobile internet entrepreneurship: launched in 2013, it took off relying on the network of acquaintances at 3W Coffee and the internet entrepreneurship boom; it became an industry star in 2015 thanks to the mass entrepreneurship policy; it sold 60% of its equity to 51job in 2017, losing its independence; after 2018, it attempted an education transformation as a second growth curve, but encountered internet layoffs and the collapse of the education and training industry; ultimately, it was forgotten in the AI recruitment era. Through Lagou's case, the article profoundly analyzes the vulnerability of vertical platforms within industry cycles—when the internet industry shifted from expansion to contraction, the myth of "programmers getting salary increases by switching jobs" that Lagou relied on also collapsed. The article also compares BOSS Zhipin, which rose during the same period, pointing out that Lagou's failure was not only a result of business competition but also the end of the era narrative represented by the "internet career advancement channel."
