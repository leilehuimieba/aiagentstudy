## Core Takeaway

This article is a candid sharing by Cheng Mengqi, founder of SophiaPro, at the Jinqiu Small Table event, providing a complete retrospective of her nearly 300-day journey in AI application entrepreneurship. Using 10 key decisions as a narrative thread, the article recounts the team's trajectory from initially choosing the vertical Agent direction, selecting a marketing scenario, making technical decisions between Agent and Workflow, to ultimately pivoting from ToB to C-end products. The founder openly analyzes the logic behind each decision and subsequent reflections, including choosing Agent over Workflow due to 'technical vanity,' deviating from market needs because of a desire to 'show off,' and the immense engineering challenges faced when building client-side products. The article also shares practical experiences such as transitioning from the browser plugin Clico to the desktop product Invoko, conducting user research via Reddit, and finding the correct ToB business model. Finally, the founder offers personal judgments on trends like OPC, the future of software, and company organization, emphasizing that 'everything that goes into production is 100 times harder than talking about concepts.'
