## Core Takeaway

This report is a systematic research outcome of a joint research group from Tencent Research Institute and Communication University of China, released in May 2026. The first part proposes 'Ten Dawn Questions,' conducting dialectical analysis around ten core issues: cost reduction and efficiency improvement, premium content creation, rise of super creators, workforce structure, skill upgrading, IP monetization, business models, cultural heritage, public aesthetics, and human-machine symbiosis. The second part, based on 444 creator-side and 1,439 consumer-side questionnaires, reveals key data such as an AI tool penetration rate of 89%, nearly 60% of creators seeing no change in average revenue per piece, and prominent consumer concerns about AI content homogenization. The third part proposes differentiated integration paths for three types of entities—traditional formats, digital content formats, and AI-native formats—as well as a new market landscape of five-party symbiosis. The report emphasizes that the industry is undergoing five structural shifts, from linear assembly lines to networked co-creation, and from capacity competition to originality competition, ultimately returning to the value of 'human as the subject.'
