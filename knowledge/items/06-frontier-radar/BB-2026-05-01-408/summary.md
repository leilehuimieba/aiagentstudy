## Core Takeaway

This article reports on a speech by Huawei Semiconductor Business President He Tingbo at IEEE ISCAS 2026, where she formally introduced the 'Tau Scaling Law.' Centered on the circuit time constant τ, this law advocates replacing traditional 'geometric scaling' with 'time scaling.' By utilizing 3D stacking technologies such as LogicFolding and backside power delivery, it aims to continuously reduce signal propagation delay, thereby opening a new path for semiconductor performance improvement as transistor miniaturization approaches physical limits. The article details the physical meaning of τ, explains why wire delay has become the primary bottleneck in current chip design, and describes how 3D stacking and backside power delivery optimize τ. Huawei revealed that over the past six years, it has designed and mass-produced 381 chips based on the Tau Scaling Law. The first Kirin chip using LogicFolding technology will be released this fall, and is expected to reach an equivalent 1.4nm process level by 2031. The article also notes that this technology will first benefit Huawei's AI computing product line.
