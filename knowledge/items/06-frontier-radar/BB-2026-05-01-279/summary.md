## Core Takeaway

The article systematically analyzes why Anthropic has stood out in the AI competition from both strategic and organizational dimensions. Strategically, Anthropic focused early on coding, abandoning side projects like multimodality, and used a 'race of wits' approach to build advantages in key battlefields. Organizationally, Anthropic has cultivated a unique culture characterized by mission-driven purpose, high trust with low ego, and a strong humanistic foundation, maintained through special hiring criteria, transparent information sharing, and a co-founder structure with equal voting rights. The article contrasts OpenAI's multi-front expansion with Anthropic's extreme focus, arguing that the latter's success stems from fewer bets, lower ego, and a naive mission.
