## Core Takeaway

This article announces a significant breakthrough in mathematics achieved by an internal OpenAI reasoning model. The model has solved the Erdős unit distance problem, a longstanding open problem in combinatorial geometry that asks for the maximum number of unit-distance pairs among n points in the plane. For decades, the prevailing belief was that the 'square grid' construction was essentially optimal. The AI model disproved this conjecture by providing an infinite family of examples that yield a polynomial improvement, achieving at least n^(1+δ) unit-distance pairs for a fixed δ > 0. The proof, which has been verified by external mathematicians, ingeniously applies sophisticated tools from algebraic number theory, such as infinite class field towers, to an elementary geometric question. This marks the first time an AI has autonomously resolved a prominent open problem central to a subfield of mathematics.
