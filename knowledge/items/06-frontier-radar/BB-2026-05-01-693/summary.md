## Core Takeaway

BBC Sport's football tactics correspondent unpacks the common patterns behind the World Cup underdog performances. The piece examines four key themes: refusing to be drawn out of shape (Cape Verde vs Spain, Ghana vs England), covering the pitch widthally (contrasting Saudi Arabia and Sweden's failed back-five systems vs Cape Verde and Ghana's compact 4-5-1), attracting pressure then playing long from short goal-kicks (South Africa vs South Korea, Iraq vs Norway), and acknowledging the role of individual quality and inspired goalkeeping. The analysis uses diagrams, PPDA data, and specific match situations to show how discipline and tactical intelligence can narrow the gap against stronger opponents.
