## Core Takeaway

This edition of AINews from Latent Space provides a comprehensive roundup of the most significant AI developments from late May 2026. Key topics include the rollout of Anthropic's Claude Opus 4.8, which shows incremental but not dominant improvements across various benchmarks, with some users praising its less over-agentic behavior. A critical finding in multi-turn RL training is highlighted: a 'Token-In, Token-Out' rule is proposed to fix a silent bug where re-tokenizing tool calls breaks gradient application. Research highlights include Bidirectional Evolutionary Search (BES) for improved reasoning, BeliefTrack for belief-state management, and new multimodal world models. The issue also features a deep dive into the new StepFun 3.7 Flash model, a 196B MoE model with strong agentic benchmarks and day-0 llama.cpp support.
