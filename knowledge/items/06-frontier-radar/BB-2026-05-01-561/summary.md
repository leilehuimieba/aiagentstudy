## Core Takeaway

This article is a transcript of an on-site conversation at the MiniMax Dev Meetup from the 'Crossing' podcast. MiniMax founder and CEO Yan Junjie (IO) engaged in an in-depth discussion with several developers on topics including the M3 model, the 10T model plan, AI Coding practices, and multimodal strategy. IO shared MiniMax's iterative journey from M1 to M3, emphasizing that general consumption is an objective metric for measuring model value, and revealed that M3 is close to the critical point where users can use Sonnet/Opus-level models without restrictions. He proposed that the 10T model is a milestone that must be conquered, but faces systemic challenges such as data quality and the failure of Scaling Law extrapolation. In terms of AI Coding, multiple guests discussed issues like code quality loss of control and verification lag brought about by Vibe Coding, emphasizing the need to return to engineering fundamentals. IO also elaborated on MiniMax's differentiated strategy of advancing both Coding and content generation, and the concept of introducing industry experts through the '10x' project to enhance model vertical capabilities. The Q&A session covered practical issues such as M3 inference speed optimization, instruction-following improvements, private deployment, and applications in the financial sector.
