## Core Takeaway

Prompted by four friends seeking advice on transitioning to an FDE role, the article systematically analyzes this rapidly rising position in the AI era. The author first points out that FDE is neither traditional consulting nor software outsourcing; its core value lies in 'probing model boundaries' rather than 'designing processes,' and in 'co-exploration' rather than 'requirement fulfillment.' The article traces two historical roots of FDE: Palantir's client-embedded model and the product deployment needs of model companies post-2023. It then analyzes the local predecessors of FDE in China (cloud vendor solution architects, new roles in AI startups) and three key differences in the local environment. Finally, the author provides profiles of those suitable and unsuitable for FDE, along with a self-assessment checklist, concluding that FDE is the first named new role form in the AI era, with more 'forward-deployed' versions likely to emerge in the future.
