## Core Takeaway

In this special episode of Latent Space, guest host Noah Smith and Brandon Anderson interview Yaroslav Azhnyuk, a Ukrainian tech entrepreneur who transitioned from building PetCube to founding The Fourth Law, a leading AI-guided drone company. The conversation covers the complete transformation of warfare driven by FPV drones, which now cause 70-80% of frontline casualties, dethroning artillery as the 'god of war'. Azhnyuk details the modern drone tech stack, including the five levels of autonomy, the trade-offs between fiber optic and AI-guided systems, and the eight dimensions of the autonomous battlefield. He argues that the cost-effectiveness of drones (a $500 drone vs. a $5 million tank) and China's massive manufacturing capacity (4 billion drones vs. Ukraine's 4 million) create a strategic vulnerability for the West. The discussion also covers the moral questions of building weapons, the challenges of electronic warfare and radio horizons, and the urgent need for Western defense policy to adapt to this new reality.
