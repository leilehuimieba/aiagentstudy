## Core Takeaway

This article provides a detailed report on the core announcements from Google I/O 2026. The keynote kicked off with the Gemini Omni model, which combines reasoning with multimodal generation to understand the physical world and generate videos, seen as a key step towards a 'world model'. Following this, the Gemini 3.5 Flash model was introduced, prioritizing speed and cost, specifically optimized for agentic coding and long-horizon tasks, with output speeds 4 times faster than frontier models. Based on this model, Antigravity 2.0 has been upgraded to a standalone desktop application, shifting to an Agent First mode. A live demo showcased 93 sub-agents building an operating system in parallel. In search, AI Mode was upgraded to Gemini 3.5, introducing the ability to create information agents and generate interactive interfaces. On the consumer side, the new Gemini Spark is a personal AI agent running in the cloud, capable of performing background tasks around the clock. Additionally, the Gemini App received a major overhaul with a new Daily Brief feature. In creative tools, Google Pics was launched, and Stitch and Flow were updated. On the hardware front, Android XR expanded to smart glasses, with the first audio glasses launching in the fall. The article concludes by noting that these powerful AI features come with immense computational costs, signaling Google's transformation from a free internet services company into an AI subscription infrastructure company.
