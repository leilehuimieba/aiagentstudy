## Core Takeaway

This article is an in-depth review by Aifaner of the AI full-sense wearable device launched by Guangfan Technology. The device consists of three components: a headset (with a camera), a charging case (with eSIM and GPS), and a smartwatch, designed to form an independent AI hardware hub separate from the smartphone. After 72 hours of experience, the reviewer categorized the core functions into three types: an intelligent schedule manager (automatically filtering messages, adding events, and reminding about commutes), visual perception (identifying products and querying information), and condition-triggered reminders (based on location and physiological data). The review notes that the product is forward-looking in concept, attempting to explore the next-generation personal terminal form. However, it has significant shortcomings in actual use: visual recognition latency is as high as 20 seconds, third-party service authorization is cumbersome, the charging case must be carried as the hub, and there is an inherent conflict between AI voice announcements and the immersive nature of headphones. The article argues that while some of these limitations can be resolved with technological advancements, the core challenge lies in the interaction fragmentation caused by using headphones as an AI assistant. The final conclusion is that the device cannot yet replace a smartphone, but it points a direction for future AI hardware.
