## Core Takeaway

Simon Willison reports on the release of Gemini 3.5 Flash at Google I/O 2026, noting it skipped the preview phase and went straight to general availability. Google is integrating the model into its key products, including the Gemini app, AI Mode in Google Search, and developer platforms like Google Antigravity and Android Studio. The model supports 1,048,576 input tokens and 65,536 output tokens, with a knowledge cut-off of January 2025. A notable change is the price increase: 3.5 Flash is 3x the cost of 3 Flash Preview and 6x the cost of 3.1 Flash-Lite, at $1.50/million input and $9/million output. This aligns with a broader industry trend of rising API prices from OpenAI and Anthropic. Willison also tests the model's image generation capabilities with an SVG prompt, producing a stylized pelican on a bicycle at a cost of roughly 13 cents. The article provides a concise, data-driven analysis of the model's specs, pricing, and deployment strategy.
