## Core Takeaway

This article provides a detailed review of Tencent's newly launched Marvis AI assistant, positioned as an 'operating system-level personal AI assistant' and developed by the team behind the 14-year-old App Store, Yingyongbao. Marvis comes with six specialized Agents (PM, File, Computer, APP, Search, Browser) that work out of the box without requiring users to build workflows. Core capabilities include deep integration with system information and configuration entries, allowing direct modification of system settings; powerful local file search supporting multi-dimensional fuzzy searches by file name, document content, and text within images; cross-device control enabling a phone to take over a computer desktop in real-time; support for custom automated tasks and Skill extensions; and two security options: Efficiency Mode (cloud-based large model) and Privacy Mode (on-device model). The article argues that Marvis represents the evolution of personal AI from a 'Q&A interface' to a 'device task scheduling layer,' making computers truly begin to understand users.
