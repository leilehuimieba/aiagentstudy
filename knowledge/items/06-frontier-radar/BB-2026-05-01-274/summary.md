## Core Takeaway

This article is a compilation of Fang Han's speech at the 2026 China AIGC Industry Summit. Drawing on his complete internet cycle experience, Fang Han offered sharp insights on employment, corporate strategy, and technology trends in the AI era. Key points include: Token consumption is becoming the electricity metric of the AI era, with a gap of several orders of magnitude between average and power users; experience is no longer a moat, and closed-loop, fault-tolerant jobs (e.g., artists, programmers, middle management) are most vulnerable to AI replacement, while high-judgment, non-fault-tolerant roles (e.g., surgery, on-site construction) remain safe; with AI's intervention, personal career ladders are compressed, causing middle-tier employees to polarize rapidly into novices and experts; five types of people AI cannot replace: storytellers, idea creators, definers of beauty, system builders, and paradigm reshapers. On corporate strategy, he recommended that most industries aim for second place in AI implementation to avoid the exploration costs of being first and the marginalization of being third, but in the IT sector, only first place matters. He also cautioned that claims from companies currently fundraising or listed about AGI's imminent arrival are merely for market valuation.
