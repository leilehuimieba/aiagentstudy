## Core Takeaway

This article is a detailed profile and interview with Aryna Sabalenka, the world No 1 in women's tennis, conducted by The Guardian ahead of Wimbledon. It opens with her shocking French Open quarter-final loss to Diana Shnaider, where she lost 10 consecutive games after being a set and a break up, leading her to say she wanted to quit tennis. She discusses her powerful playing style (fastest forehand and serve in the women's game), her acceptance of emotional outbursts as a necessary release, and her transformation into a more controlled player while still being authentic. The article also touches on her engagement, her expensive jewellery, and her off-court personality—different from the fierce competitor on court. Throughout, the journalist presents a nuanced portrait of a complex athlete who is self-aware, funny, and warm in person, despite her reputation for implosions.
