## Core Takeaway

This article provides a detailed report on the core content of Meta's 2026 annual shareholder meeting. The meeting rejected all ten shareholder proposals, including those on AI data usage risk reports, abolishing the dual-class share structure, and child safety, with the highest support rate reaching only 27%.
