## Core Takeaway

ElevenLabs has launched Music v2, a significant upgrade to its AI music generation model. The new model delivers enhanced quality in vocals, instrumentation, and arrangement across all genres. Key new capabilities include improved inpainting for regenerating specific sections of a track, the ability to compose full songs section-by-section (intro, verse, chorus) while maintaining structural coherence, and better multilingual support for lyrics and vocals. Music v2 powers three distinct platforms: ElevenMusic for creators, ElevenAPI for developers, and ElevenCreative for brands. Alongside the release, ElevenLabs has reduced pricing by up to 50% for API users and 40% for Creative customers. The model is trained on licensed data and cleared for commercial use, with no sync fees or clearance delays.
