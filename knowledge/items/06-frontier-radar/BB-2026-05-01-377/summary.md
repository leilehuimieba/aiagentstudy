## Core Takeaway

This article is an in-depth interview with Lee Kai-Fu, CEO of 01.AI, by LatePost. It details the company's transformation journey after abandoning pre-training large models in early 2025 and merging some teams into Alibaba. Lee Kai-Fu candidly shares the initial morale crisis, employee departures, and how he rebuilt team confidence through town hall meetings and securing the first major deal. The core transformation strategy is the 'Number One Project,' where Lee Kai-Fu personally acts as the 'Chief AI Strategy Officer' to open doors for enterprise leaders, providing a full suite of enterprise AI transformation tools based on Multi-Agent and ontology. The article discloses 01.AI's financials: audited revenue of 250 million RMB in 2025, orders exceeding 1.5 billion RMB in 2026, preparations for an IPO, and plans to become China's first profitable AI 2.0 company next year. Lee Kai-Fu also shares his views on the competitive landscape of large models, predicting consolidation among foundation model companies and emphasizing that the real opportunity lies in helping enterprises use AI to change their financial metrics.
