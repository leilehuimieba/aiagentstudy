## Core Takeaway

This article is the full text of an academic report invited by the Taotian Meta Technology Team at the China Conference on Image and Graphics (CCIG 2026). The article first outlines the diverse styles and application scenarios of digital humans, then details the three core capabilities of the TaoAvatar technology system: TaoModel (3DGS human body static modeling), TaoAvatar (3DGS drivable digital human reconstruction), and TaoVideo (4DGS human volumetric video). In the core technology section, the article elaborates on the realistic digital human technology framework based on 3D Gaussian Splatting, including realistic body and motion capture (based on SMPLX++ extension), realistic dynamic reconstruction (unified driving for multiple garments), and realistic speech-driven gesture algorithms (GestureDiT + LLM). The article also introduces the TaoVideo-4DGS volumetric video solution, which uses spline curves to describe Gaussian point motion and appearance, achieving complex dynamic effects. In terms of application, the article showcases the practices of Taobao Vision in its online App (winner of the Apple Design Awards) and offline future flagship stores, as well as brand collaborations such as the Berghaus virtual clothing store and the digital human for the Milan Winter Olympics uniforms. Finally, the article looks ahead to future technological evolution directions, including a low-cost digital human generation solution based on a single image/sparse views (FHAvatar), and introduces the team and recruitment information.
