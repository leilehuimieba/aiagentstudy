## Core Takeaway

This article is a curated compilation of Founder Park's interview with YC partner Pete Koomen. Pete shares the complete journey of YC's internal AI Agent infrastructure development over the past year. Starting with a SQL query Agent for the finance team, the system has evolved into an internal registry of 350+ tools, a company-wide visible Agent conversation system, and a Dream Cycle that automatically reads all conversations every night to improve its own skills. The article delves into the fundamental difference between an AI-native organization and one that merely 'uses AI as a Copilot': the former treats AI as the building layer of the organization, systematically recording all artifacts (meeting recordings, Agent conversations, decision documents) as raw material for Agent learning. Pete and Gary emphasize that achieving this requires two cultural prerequisites: Egalitarian (AI capabilities open to everyone) and Trust by default (all Agent conversations are visible company-wide). Additionally, the article discusses the design philosophy of the tool registry and skill system, the demand explosion driven by cost reduction (Jevons paradox), and the future software paradigm of 'Agent wrapping deterministic tools'.
