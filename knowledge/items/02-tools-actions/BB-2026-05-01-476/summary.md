## Core Takeaway

This article announces that VoidZero, the team behind the foundational JavaScript toolchain including Vite, Vitest, Rolldown, and Oxc, is joining Cloudflare. The core message is that all projects will remain MIT-licensed, open source, vendor-agnostic, and community-driven. Cloudflare commits engineering resources and a $1 million Vite ecosystem fund. The article details the existing collaboration on the Vite Environment API and Cloudflare Vite plugin, and outlines a future where Vite becomes a full-stack foundation for modern applications, including AI-agent-coded ones. Cloudflare plans to build its own developer tooling (like the new `cf` CLI) on top of Vite, rather than moving Vite towards Cloudflare. The long-term vision includes open-sourcing the Void platform and adding provider-agnostic primitives for full-stack apps and agents to Vite itself.
