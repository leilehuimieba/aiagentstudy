## Core Takeaway

Based on real practices from the author's company (a team of about 30 people), this article details how to leverage Feishu CLI's open capabilities to enable Agents like Claude Code to directly control Feishu for office automation. Key cases include: 1) Automatically fetching meeting notes data for recurring meetings (topic selection meetings, training sessions) to build a cross-session knowledge base; 2) Agents pulling data in parallel from Feishu private messages, group chats, meetings, meeting notes, schedules, tasks, emails, documents, and OKRs to automatically generate personal quarterly work retrospective reports; 3) Automating the reconciliation process, where an Agent bot pulls data from multi-dimensional tables, interacts with bloggers in a group, and automatically routes tasks to business or finance teams based on feedback; 4) Using Feishu's whiteboard capabilities, Agents directly generate architecture diagrams, flowcharts, and even PPTs that can be collaboratively edited by the entire team; 5) Agents automatically extracting invoices from Feishu email, filling in and submitting expense reports according to the standard operating procedure (SOP), with the approval end also handled by an Agent after verifying the information. The article emphasizes that Feishu CLI now covers 15 business domains and 114 capabilities, and the deep integration of Agents with Feishu is reshaping team collaboration.
