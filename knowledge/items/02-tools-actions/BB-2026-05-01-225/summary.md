## Core Takeaway

This article is an in-depth investment analysis report on Supabase. It points out that Supabase's success stems from its precise positioning at the intersection of two major AI-era trends: Postgres has become the 'mental language' for AI Agents, and Coding represents the biggest explosion point for AI demand. The article breaks down Supabase's product roadmap, arguing that it has shifted from 'providing a better experience for human developers' to 'providing better Postgres capabilities for Agents.' Through acquisitions of companies like OrioleDB, Multigres, Hydra, and BKND, Supabase is addressing the scalability bottlenecks of single-node Postgres, enhancing analytical capabilities, and laying the groundwork for an Agent-native lightweight backend. The article also analyzes its growth drivers (integration with Vibe Coding platforms and automatic Agent recommendations), competitive landscape (cloud vendors, Neon, Convex), and key challenges (enterprise market adoption, unit economics). Finally, it evaluates Supabase's team, financial data, and TAM, concluding that it possesses a formidable distribution moat and significant platformization potential.
