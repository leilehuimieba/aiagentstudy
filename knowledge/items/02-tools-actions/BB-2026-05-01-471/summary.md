## Core Takeaway

This article is the complete transcript of a talk by Daniela Amodei, co-founder and president of Anthropic, at Stanford University's "View from the Top" interview series. With an English literature background and a non-traditional path through Capitol Hill, political campaigns, and Stripe before entering the AI industry, she argues for the unique value of generalists in the AI field. The interview revolves around seven themes: how generalists can enter AI, the founding logic of Anthropic (running towards something, not away), defining safety as "radical responsibility" in business decisions (including proactively delaying the Mythos model release), AI's impact on employment (complementarity over replacement), higher optimism about AI in the Global South, the risk of cognitive outsourcing (humans voluntarily giving up thinking), and the re-pricing of human skills (empathy, explanation, judgment, relationships) in the AI era. Using a doctor's bedside manner as an example, she illustrates how soft skills are becoming core value indicators. The interview also covers student questions on the AI bubble, government regulation, and personal privacy.
