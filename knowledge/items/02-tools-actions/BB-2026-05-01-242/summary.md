## Core Takeaway

The article introduces "Marvis", an AI Agent product launched by the Tencent App Store team, positioned as an operating system-level AI assistant. Unlike traditional AI assistants, Marvis is deeply integrated into the system's underlying architecture, enabling it to directly perform tasks such as file management, document parsing, system setting modifications (e.g., disabling Windows ads), and hardware configuration checks without requiring manual user intervention. Its core differentiator lies in deep collaborations with companies like Microsoft, Intel, and Qualcomm, granting it low-level system access. It employs a routing mechanism to automatically assign tasks to local or cloud-based models and supports a privacy mode that runs entirely on-device. The product also includes a cartoon-style Agent studio with multiple preset roles working collaboratively. The team emphasizes that Marvis is a product that "grew" organically rather than being a conceptual design, and future iterations will continue to focus on its positioning as a system-level AI assistant.
