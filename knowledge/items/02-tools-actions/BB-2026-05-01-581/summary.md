## Core Takeaway

Based on the author's hands-on experience with the eight products released at the Meitu Imaging Festival, the article analyzes Meitu's latest strategic shift in the AI imaging field. It highlights Picchi (which learns personal retouching styles from user-uploaded before-and-after photos or borrows aesthetics from influencers), MVLAND (which deeply understands musical structures to automatically generate matching MVs), Kaipai (which fully auto-edits talking videos into finished clips), Meitu Design Studio (which generates a full set of e-commerce materials from a single product image), and RoboNeo (a tool for scaled short drama production). The article points out that unlike industry-standard AI tools that emphasize "generation capabilities," Meitu this time focuses on "delivering results." By building an "AI imaging team" composed of specialized Agents, it allows users to get finished products directly without learning complex tool operations. The author believes this approach of "making AI adapt to people and being responsible for the results" elevates the baseline of AI products from "generation" to "delivery."
