## Core Takeaway

Taking Zhipu's market cap surpassing one trillion HKD as a starting point, the article analyzes the company's 2025 financial report (revenue of 724 million yuan, net loss of 4.718 billion yuan, R&D expenditure of 3.18 billion yuan) and the technical performance of its GLM-5.2 model to explain why the market continues to push the stock price higher despite the clearly excessive valuation. The author identifies three underlying logics: a dual scarcity effect formed by the limited supply of Coding Plan on the product side and an extremely low free float (only about 2.67% of H-shares are tradable) on the capital side; concentrated passive buying triggered by inclusion in the Hang Seng Tech Index; and the scarcity narrative around foundational AI model companies. The article also references Keynes's beauty contest theory and Soros's reflexivity concept to analyze how prices become self-reinforcing. It warns of risks: the unlocking of restricted shares in July and slowing quarterly growth could lead to a valuation collapse, as well as potential competitive threats from DeepSeek and others. Finally, it points out that the risk-reward ratio at current prices is severely asymmetric, and whether Zhipu can transform its high valuation into an infrastructure platform position will be the key to long-term success.
