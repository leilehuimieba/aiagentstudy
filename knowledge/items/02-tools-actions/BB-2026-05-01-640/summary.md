## Core Takeaway

The article documents the author's complete experience with WeChat's in-testing AI assistant 'WeChat Xiaowei'. From a product manager's viewpoint, the author notes that Xiaowei continues WeChat's long-standing product philosophy: non-intrusive, easy to use, seamless integration into the ecosystem, and starting with basic features. The core functionality is mini-tool generation – users can describe a need via voice or text, and Xiaowei generates a dedicated mini-program (currently for personal use only). The author probed and discovered that WeChat has a rich set of internal APIs but permissions are not yet opened. They also tested the scheduled message feature and unexpectedly encountered Xiaowei's firm stance on privacy and ethics (refusing to bypass the recipient's block and delivering moral lectures on deception). The article concludes by speculating that WeChat is building a new system based on a mobile phone and a single app with Xiaowei as the foundation, where traditional communication functions may just be a subset.
