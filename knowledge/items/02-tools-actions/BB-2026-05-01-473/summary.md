## Core Takeaway

This edition of AINews from Latent Space covers the most significant AI developments from June 2-3, 2026. The top story is Microsoft's release of the MAI-Thinking-1 technical report, a 109-page document detailing a reasoning model trained without third-party distillation, achieving 97% on AIME 2025 and 53% on SWE-Bench Pro. The report was praised for its unusual transparency, including scaling ladder recipes, MFU numbers, and training data composition. Microsoft also announced Frontier Tuning, a customization infrastructure for enterprise workflows. In open models, Google launched Gemma 4 12B, an Apache 2.0 multimodal model with an encoder-free design for on-device use, while Ideogram 4.0 switched to open weights, becoming the top-ranked open image model on the Arena leaderboard. The agent ecosystem is shifting from frameworks to execution harnesses and multi-agent DAG-based systems, with concrete gains reported. A significant debate emerged around model routing, with some arguing it's inevitable for cost control and others calling it 'snake oil.' Enterprise cost ceilings are becoming real, with Uber reportedly capping coding-agent spend at $1,500/month per employee. The roundup also covers open audio models (Miso One, Fun-Realtime-TTS) and the broader trend of local AI becoming a mainstream deployment target.
