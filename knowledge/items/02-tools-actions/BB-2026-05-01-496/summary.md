## Core Takeaway

Based on exclusive reporting from the Financial Times, the article details OpenAI's largest redesign of ChatGPT since its 2022 launch. The core change involves integrating Codex, its fastest-growing programming tool, into ChatGPT and connecting it with external partner applications. The analysis unfolds across four dimensions: strategic intent, technological foundation, competitive pressure, and business logic. OpenAI executives believe the future of AI lies in agents that execute tasks for users, not simple conversations. The upgrade of the underlying GPT-5.5 model enables Codex to independently perform complex, multi-step tasks. Anthropic's Claude Code, which previously led OpenAI in coding capabilities, directly prompted OpenAI to rapidly form a Codex team and restructure its organization. With an IPO approaching, OpenAI needs to push Codex to its 900 million consumer users to drive willingness to pay. The article also notes that Codex's desktop app saw its user base grow sixfold in two months, surpassing 5 million weekly active users, and details OpenAI's decision to shelve consumer businesses like Sora to concentrate resources on enterprise clients.
