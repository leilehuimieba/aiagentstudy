## Core Takeaway

Based on internal practices at Alibaba, this article provides a comprehensive guide to developing and managing AI Agent Skills. It begins with the essence of Skill (a structured instruction document) and an analogy (developing an operations manual), explaining its three-level loading mechanism in detail. Subsequently, the article covers the Skill directory structure, the YAML header and Markdown body writing standards for the SKILL.md file, and provides a complete workflow from installation to release. It deeply analyzes three major pain points in the Skill ecosystem: cross-platform consistency, version management, and development/debugging efficiency, offering specific strategies and best practices. Finally, the article announces the launch of the one-stop Skill development assistant, skill-dev-aio, which integrates features like creation, publishing, evaluation, querying, migration, and batch updates, aiming to lower the barrier to Skill development and let developers focus on experience and judgment.

## Reusable Principle

A practical guide for developers turning repeated workflows into reusable Agent Skills. It explains trigger design, file structure, authoring conventions, release, debugging, evaluation, and cross-platform migration, then ties those ideas to the skill-dev-aio assistant. Read it if you want a concrete operating model for building Skills rather than another abstract prompt-engineering essay.
