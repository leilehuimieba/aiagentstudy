## Core Takeaway

This article summarizes the key developer announcements from the Google I/O 2026 keynote. The central theme is the transition from AI assistants to autonomous agents. Major announcements include the Gemini 3.5 series of models and the upgraded Antigravity 2.0 platform, which features a new CLI, orchestration for subagents, and built-in security. For Android development, Google introduced the Android CLI and open-sourced Android skills for agents, a Migration agent to convert cross-platform code to native Kotlin, and the Android Bench leaderboard. For web development, they proposed the WebMCP open standard for browser-based agents, launched Modern Web Guidance for coding agents, and introduced Chrome DevTools for agents and the HTML-in-Canvas API. The article positions these tools as a unified ecosystem for building agent-driven applications.
