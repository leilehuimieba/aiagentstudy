## Core Takeaway

Based on Purple Technology's (Purple Bird Browser) Hong Kong IPO prospectus, this article breaks down its financial and business substance one by one. Data shows that out of total revenue of 687 million RMB in 2025, Purple Bird Browser contributed 84.1%, while AI product LinkFox accounted for only 2.5%; operating profit was 257 million RMB and operating cash flow was 364 million RMB, but net profit was only 57.49 million RMB, mainly due to redeemable liability book fluctuations. ARPU, retention rate, and renewal rate all declined year by year, with growth relying on new subscriber volume and the acquisition of Zhanfu Browser. Before the IPO, the company distributed 240 million RMB in dividends in a rush and hoarded 600 million RMB in wealth management products, while R&D intensity actually declined. Founder Yang Jinhe came from a seller background, and the team scrambled to build a governance structure before going public. The article argues that the company is essentially an 'anti-detect browser' business, with a gap between AI narrative and investment, and highlights risks deeply tied to platform policies.
