## Core Takeaway

This article is the author's technical retrospective and product reflection on their open-source project Huashu-Design 2.0. The project has garnered over 16k stars on GitHub and is positioned as an AI-driven design agent capable of generating high-quality visual outputs such as websites, app prototypes, PPTs, and animations. The author candidly analyzes three core flaws of v1: 1) Design styles converged towards 'safe minimalism,' leading to repetitive outputs; 2) Content-driven websites lacked real images, becoming empty shells; 3) AI fabricated product facts based on training data, resulting in unusable designs. To address these issues, v2 introduces three parallel design logics (random collision, learning from award-winning works, simulating top designer thinking) to achieve stylistic diversity; adds an 'image-first' step to ensure content-driven designs have real material support; and prioritizes 'fact verification before assumption' as the highest principle to avoid factual errors. The article also discusses the advantages of HTML Deck over traditional PPT and the applicable boundaries of the project. Overall, it is an insightful, case-driven, and original practical sharing piece.
