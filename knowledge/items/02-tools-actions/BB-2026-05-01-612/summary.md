## Core Takeaway

This article details the complete evolution path of De Wu's community event creation system from form-driven to Agent-driven. The first version used AI to assist form filling, but the paradigm remained unchanged, resulting in limited efficiency gains. The second version, based on LangGraph, orchestrated workflows with interrupt/resume mechanisms, transforming operators from process executors to supervisors, and designed a component module protocol to address scalability. The third version further split into two stages: 'Plan Generation Skill' and 'Aggregation Workbench', supporting the full chain from idea to planning to venue creation, along with natural language editing and visual preview. The article discusses key decisions such as Workflow vs Agent selection, Human-in-the-loop interaction design, side effect control, and engineering compromises, concluding with three unchanging principles: controllable process, correctness priority, and lower operational burden. The article offers highly valuable engineering insights for AI product deployment.
