## Core Takeaway

The article, originally created by the author's 'Guizang's AI Toolbox', shares the design philosophy and core features of the open-source project guizang-social-card-skill. Positioned as a small product rather than a simple prompt, this Skill aims to address the pain points of content creators on graphic platforms like Xiaohongshu. Its core highlights include: built-in adaptation rules for 11 graphic categories (e.g., travel, workplace, film reviews) that automatically match visual styles based on content; solving the challenge of text overlay in AI-generated images through subject recognition, color brightness calculation, and adaptive layout for a premium feel; default integration with free, commercially usable image libraries like Pexels and Unsplash to avoid watermarks and homogeneity issues from AI-generated images; and features like screenshot beautification, two visual systems (magazine style/grid style), and 28 layout templates to ensure output stability. The author emphasizes that the Skill's moat lies in its product-oriented thinking, including clear boundary definitions, version management, and delivery documentation, aiming to upgrade the Skill from a one-time prompt to a reusable product.
