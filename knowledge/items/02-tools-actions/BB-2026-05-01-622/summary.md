## Core Takeaway

This article is compiled from a roundtable discussion at the Singapore Agent Summit, where five VCs (Golden Gate Ventures, Openspace Capital, 59st Ventures, Monk's Hill Ventures, Accel) shared their core criteria for evaluating AI Agent startups. The article first points out that most companies remain at the "curious" demo stage and fail to create "dependency"—meaning no one notices when the Agent disappears. The investors emphasize that the true moat is the "data flywheel" (proprietary data + iterative optimization), rather than static data; surviving companies must make Agents irreplaceable (replacing employee tasks), have quantifiable ROI (key metrics drop significantly when the Agent is unavailable), and deeply integrate into customer workflows. Additionally, globalization should start from Day 1, using the PESTLE framework to select the first market and focusing on a single pain point to the extreme. The five VCs also provided their respective "startup first aid kit" keywords, such as globalization, speed, resilience, unique insights, and data flywheel. The article concludes with "sleeping under the customer's desk for two weeks," emphasizing that entrepreneurs must be extremely obsessed with customer pain points.
