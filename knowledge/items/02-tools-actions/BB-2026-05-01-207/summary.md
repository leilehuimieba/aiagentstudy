## Core Takeaway

This article introduces OpenAI's new remote control feature for Codex, which allows users to remotely connect to and control Codex running on their home computer through the ChatGPT app on their phone. The author details the setup process: enabling mobile settings in Codex on the computer, generating a QR code, scanning it with the phone to authorize login, and achieving real-time synchronization between the phone and computer. Once connected, the phone can send new tasks, view worker threads, check outputs, approve commands, and more. All files, credentials, and permissions remain on the local computer, with communication secured by a relay layer. The author compares this experience with Claude Code's remote control, finding Codex to have fewer bugs and a smoother experience, and shares a real-world case of modifying a project while traveling. The article also outlines other recent important updates to Codex, including Computer Use (background computer control), a built-in browser (supporting direct annotations and comments), a Chrome extension (leveraging logged-in browser state to interact with websites), and integration with gpt-image-2 for generating images directly within code. The author believes the competition between Codex and Claude Code mirrors the golden age of iOS vs. Android and recommends using both to adapt to changes.
