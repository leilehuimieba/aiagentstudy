## Core Takeaway

This article is an interpretation of the survey paper "Token Economics for LLM Agents: A Dual-View Study from Computing and Economics" jointly released by Zhejiang University and Alibaba Cloud. The paper points out that as LLM Agents move into high-stakes scenarios such as finance, law, and healthcare, tokens have evolved from a technical unit into a core resource affecting system cost, latency, security, and scalability. The paper proposes a Dual-View framework that links computer science Agent optimization problems with economic theories (factors of production, medium of exchange, unit of account) and constructs a four-dimensional classification system: single-agent token efficiency (trade-offs between reasoning and tool invocation), multi-agent collaboration costs (communication and division of labor friction), Agent ecosystem pricing and governance (platform economy and Jevons paradox), and the security dimension (equilibrium between defense expenditure and attack losses). The paper also outlines six major trends and five emerging opportunities, including differentiable token budgets, real-time token markets, and agent-level token scaling laws.
