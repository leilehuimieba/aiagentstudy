## Core Takeaway

This article reports that Notion will fully shut down Notion Mail on September 22, 2026, with the product lasting only 17 months from launch to closure. The piece traces Notion Mail's origins—the acquisition of Skiff, aimed at challenging Google Workspace, with deep AI integration at its core (natural language labeling, automated email handling, etc.). The direct reason for the shutdown is that over half of users now manage email through Agents without ever opening their inboxes. The article quotes product leadership's statements and analyzes Notion's strategic calculus: calendars require GUIs due to their highly visual nature, while email is fundamentally text that can be handled by Agents—thus calendars are retained while the email client is abandoned. The article also observes the emerging "Mail for AI" trend—email services designed specifically for Agents (such as AgentMail, QQ Mail Agent Mail), where Agents possess independent identities and communication capabilities, no longer requiring a human interface. Finally, it raises a deeper question: as Agents become the primary actors, should productivity software be designed for humans or for Agents? The article maintains a clear stance, combining factual reporting with independent judgment, offering valuable insight into AI product trends.
