NEW Claude Design Hidden ‘Tweaks’ Properties!

Claude Design is evolving rapidly, and one of its most powerful yet underrated features is the Tweaks panel. This customizable modal allows designers to take manual control over their UI components, moving beyond simple prompting into precise design manipulation.

Customizing Your Workspace

The standout feature of the Tweaks panel is its ability to be extended through AI prompting. Instead of relying on the default settings, you can ask Claude to add specific controls—such as a corner radius slider—directly to the interface. This transforms the AI from a static generator into a dynamic tool that builds its own UI based on your workflow needs.

Boosting Efficiency and Saving Usage

One of the practical benefits of using these manual controls is efficiency. By having sliders for style, shape, and layout at your fingertips, you can:

Make instant adjustments to design parameters.
Reduce the number of AI prompts sent for minor fixes.
Preserve your message quota for more complex design tasks.
Understanding Usage and Limits

As Claude Design is currently in research preview, its usage tracking is unique. It requires a paid Claude plan (Pro or Enterprise), but the limits are independent of your standard Claude chat usage. These limits are reset weekly and vary by user and plan type.

To keep track of your remaining quota, navigate to the Settings > Usage section in the Claude desktop or web app. There, you'll find a dedicated progress bar for the research preview, helping you manage your design sessions effectively.
