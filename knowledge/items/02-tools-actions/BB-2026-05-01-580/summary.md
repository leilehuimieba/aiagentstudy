## Core Takeaway

Starting from the common pain point that 'phone AI assistants cannot operate WeChat,' the article reports on WeChat's collaboration with manufacturers like Huawei, Xiaomi, Honor, and OPPO to launch the A2A assistant capability. Through actual testing on the Honor 500 Pro, it showcases the smooth experience of 'sending messages, making calls, opening scan/payment codes by voice,' but also notes that sending images/emojis and transfers are not yet supported. The author clearly distinguishes between two technical paths—A2A and OCR screen-reading + simulated clicks—pointing out that A2A better balances data security, user retention, and token cost sharing. The article also looks ahead to A2A's expansion to WeChat mini-programs, other apps, and even international giants (e.g., Google's Gemini Spark, Apple's AI Siri), while cautiously highlighting practical challenges like computing costs, nested call efficiency losses, and the human risk of 'alienation.' The content is well-informed, with comprehensive background and focused analysis without overstatement.
