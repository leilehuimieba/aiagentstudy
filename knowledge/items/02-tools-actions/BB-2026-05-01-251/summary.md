## Core Takeaway

This article provides a detailed report on the 2026 Alibaba Cloud Summit, the most signal-dense press conference in Alibaba Cloud's 17-year history. Alibaba Cloud announced the completion of a full-stack Agentic upgrade from chips to inference. Key releases include: T-Head's new generation training-inference integrated AI chip Zhenwu M890 (3x performance of its predecessor) and the Panjiu AL128 supernode server; Alibaba Cloud CTO Li Feifei's release of 32 Agentic Cloud new products covering six major capabilities: runtime, orchestration, governance, security, memory, and data plane, with cloud products undergoing Skillification, MCPification, and CLIfication; the flagship model Qwen3.7-Max topping the Arena rankings domestically and autonomously working for 35 hours on the Zhenwu M890 chip to complete kernel optimization; the Bailian platform opening access to over 150 models; and the launch of the new AI product Qianwen Cloud's official website, replacing traditional navigation with a single line of instruction.
