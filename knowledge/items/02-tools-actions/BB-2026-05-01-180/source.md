- Browser title: Skill Factory: Build a Harness-Designed Skill Factory in Three Days (with AI Coding Practice)
- Description: Starting from the author's full-stack development practice with AI coding assistance, this article analyzes the pain points of two current mainstream skill generation methods (manual writing and conversational generation): low efficiency, high quality fluctuation, and lack of an automated verification closed loop. To address these issues, the author proposes the Skill Factory solution, with the core idea being 'Test-Driven Skill Generation.' This solution comprises four key stages: baseline diagnosis (evaluating whether the bare model and existing skills meet requirements), multi-path parallel generation (invoking multiple strategies/models in parallel to generate skills, improving the first-time success rate), automated test regression (scoring and optimizing from dimensions such as format specification, reuse innovation, and functional availability), and quality inspection. Additionally, the article introduces ecosystem adaptation with the Zhiliu platform and an iterative direction based on Trace2Skill, aiming to precipitate reusable skills from execution traces. Finally, the author shares practical experiences using AI coding tools (such as idealab, Qoderwork, Aimashi, and Lingma) for auxiliary design during development and compares the pros and cons of the three skill generation modes.
