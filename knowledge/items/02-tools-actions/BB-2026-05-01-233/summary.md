## Core Takeaway

Cursor has released Composer 2.5, a significant upgrade to its AI coding agent. The new version demonstrates marked improvements in handling long-running, complex tasks, following intricate instructions, and overall collaboration quality. The improvements stem from three key training innovations: targeted reinforcement learning with textual feedback to correct localized errors, a 25x increase in synthetic task generation grounded in real codebases to push model intelligence, and a new Sharded Muon optimizer with dual-mesh HSDP for efficient large-scale training. Composer 2.5 is built on the same open-source Kimi K2.5 checkpoint as its predecessor. The blog post also details a future collaboration with SpaceXAI to train a significantly larger model from scratch. Pricing is set at $0.50/M input and $2.50/M output tokens, with a faster variant available at a higher cost.
