## Core Takeaway

Simon Willison argues that Anthropic and OpenAI have finally achieved product-market fit, driven by the success of their coding agent products like Claude Code and Codex. He presents several pieces of evidence: both companies have shifted enterprise pricing from flat-rate seats to API-based usage, leading to significantly higher costs for heavy users. This is supported by stories of companies like Uber exceeding their AI budgets, which Willison interprets as a sign of genuine demand rather than failure. He also notes the massive inference spending by labs, such as Anthropic's $1.25 billion per month deal with SpaceX, and the increasing proportion of enterprise-focused job openings at both companies. Willison concludes that April 2026 marks a new inflection point where the revenue implications of these powerful agent tools are becoming clear, benefiting the AI labs and impacting corporate budgets.
