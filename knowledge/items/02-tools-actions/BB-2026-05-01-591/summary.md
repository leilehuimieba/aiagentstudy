## Core Takeaway

This is an in-depth report from Silicon Valley 101 on their visit to Beijing's Moonshot Academy. Through a two-day immersive experience, the article presents a comprehensive view of the school's educational philosophy and practices from the perspectives of its founder, teachers, and students. Founded by 19-year-old Wang Xiqiao, Moonshot Academy aims to cultivate 'individuals with inner abundance and active citizens.' The school has no traditional classrooms or fixed schedules. Its high school phase features the 'One Small Step' program, supporting student entrepreneurship (for example, Jiang Yin's project creating assistive tools for dyslexia has registered a company with a team of over 60 people). The curriculum emphasizes judgment, aesthetics, and humanities, complemented by practical spaces like a design studio and MakerSpace. The middle and elementary schools build foundational skills through project-based learning and a mentorship system. The article also objectively mentions external controversies regarding its high tuition fees and less prominent college admission results. Overall, it presents a cutting-edge exploration of educational transformation in the AI era—not training exam machines or study-abroad candidates, but enabling high schoolers to grow into 'super individuals' through real-world projects.
