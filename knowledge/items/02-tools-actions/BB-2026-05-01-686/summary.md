## Core Takeaway

Dreame Technology, known for vacuum cleaners, has rapidly expanded into diverse sectors including EVs, humanoid robots, smartphones, and satellites. Founder Yu Hao funds this expansion primarily through local government investment funds, which account for over 40% of Dreame's financing. His flamboyant marketing (Super Bowl ads, Cristiano Ronaldo endorsement) and bold claims (aiming for a $100 trillion company) appeal to local officials seeking to foster star enterprises. Sky Factory, Dreame's VC arm, manages $6.6 billion in assets from at least 18 cities. However, this model is now under scrutiny: China's State Council issued Document 54 to regulate government funds, and local governments are reviewing their deals with Dreame. Critics question whether Dreame's ecosystem-building is sustainable or a risky diversification. Former employees describe a culture of rapid hiring and layoffs, and pressure to raise money from local governments by making ambitious claims. The report provides a detailed look at the intersection of ambitious entrepreneurship and China's local government investment landscape.
