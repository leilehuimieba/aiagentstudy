## Core Takeaway

Geir Jordet, a soccer psychology expert, and his team compiled a dataset of 535 players' penalty records from official senior matches, filtering by conversion rate, volume, and other factors. The top 10 list prioritizes recent form, technical flexibility (goalkeeper-dependent vs. independent), and pressure performance. Notable entries include Harry Kane (1st) for his adaptive technique, Viktor Gyokeres (2nd) for pure power, and Raul Jimenez (3rd) for an astonishing 97.4% success rate. The article explains why global stars Messi (78%) and Ronaldo (declining 76.6%) are omitted, and provides deep tactical breakdowns for each player, including vulnerabilities.
