# Learning From Failure: Integrating Negative Examples when Fine-tuning Large Language Models as Agents

## Abstract

The paper argues that unsuccessful trajectories contain valuable information for agent tuning. Instead of discarding failures, it conditions training on whether a trajectory should succeed and shows this can improve downstream agent behavior.

## Key Evidence

- Failed trajectories are treated as useful training data rather than waste.
- A simple prefix/suffix indicating success or failure is enough to improve performance.
- The paper emphasizes quality control to balance useful information against noisy errors.
