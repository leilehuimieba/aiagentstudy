- Description: This playbook from Anthropic is a practical guide for founders building AI-native startups. It reimagines the four core stages of the startup lifecycle—Idea, MVP, Launch, and Scale—for what is possible with AI in 2026. For each stage, it outlines specific goals, exit criteria, common failure modes, and AI-powered exercises. The playbook covers validating problem hypotheses, running customer discovery with AI, architecting AI-generated codebases to avoid technical debt, measuring genuine product-market fit, and replacing founder attention with agentic workflows. It also includes a product matrix for using different Claude products (Chat, Cowork, Code) across the startup journey and features founder stories from companies like Ambral, Anything, and Carta Healthcare. The core thesis is that the founder's role is shifting from individual contributor to orchestrator, allowing them to focus on the work only they can do.
