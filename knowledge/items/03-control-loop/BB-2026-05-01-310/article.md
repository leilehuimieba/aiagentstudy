## Abstract

CRITIC lets an LLM validate and progressively amend its own outputs by interacting with external tools. Starting from an initial answer, the model critiques the output with tool feedback and then revises it.

## Key Evidence

- The paper explicitly targets flawed code, hallucinated facts, and other bad outputs.
- The correction loop depends on external validation tools rather than pure self-talk.
- It shows consistent improvement on free-form QA, math program synthesis, and toxicity reduction.
