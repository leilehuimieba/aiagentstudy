## Abstract

LATS integrates Monte Carlo Tree Search with language-model reasoning, acting, and planning. It uses LM-powered value functions, self-reflections, and external environment feedback to support deliberate exploration and adaptive decision-making.

## Key Evidence

- The framework explicitly addresses exploration over multiple candidate trajectories.
- It uses value estimates and self-reflection to improve branching decisions.
- The paper reports strong gains across programming, QA, web navigation, and math.
