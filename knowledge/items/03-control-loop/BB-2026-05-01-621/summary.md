## Core Takeaway

This article is an exclusive in-depth interview with Cao Yue, founder of Sand.ai, by The Intelligent Surge. It first reviews Sand.ai's development over nearly three years, including its bet on the autoregressive approach (Magi-1), the pioneering achievement of synchronous audio-video output (Gaga-1), and the non-consensual decision to shift to the MoE architecture (next-generation model). Cao Yue explains in detail the impossible triangle of cost, speed, and quality in video models, and argues that MoE is the key breakthrough. Amid the current overuse of the term 'world model,' Cao Yue offers a clear definition: the world model is still in its pre-GPT era, lacking sufficient data and a clear definition; video data, which encodes time, space, vision, and sound, is the most important data type for achieving the world model. He also advocates modeling raw observations rather than human-defined states to avoid repeating the failures of tokenization. On the application front, Sand.ai has launched the music agent product VidMuse, achieving millions of dollars in ARR, and insists on a dual-drive strategy of models and products. Finally, Cao Yue provides independent judgments on the competitive landscape (five players in three tiers), the reasons for Sora's shutdown, and China's advantages in video models. The article is dense with information, presents clear technical opinions, and incorporates strategic thinking.
