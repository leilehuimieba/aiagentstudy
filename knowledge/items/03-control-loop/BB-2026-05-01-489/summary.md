## Core Takeaway

This Stratechery Update provides a critical analysis of three major announcements from Computex 2026 and Microsoft Build 2026. First, Thompson argues that Nvidia's RTX Spark (N1X) chip, while powerful, is a product of a bygone AI era (ChatGPT-style chatbots) and is ill-suited for the current agentic era which demands strong CPU performance for local orchestration and cloud-based inference. He suggests Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella shares this skepticism, viewing Windows as a legacy platform. Second, Thompson is intrigued by Microsoft's Project Solara, a vaporware platform for new form-factor devices that run AI agents on Android, not Windows. He sees the core idea—a cloud-centric, multi-device agent ecosystem—as a compelling vision, especially for enterprise, that correctly positions the cloud as the hub and devices as spokes. Finally, he analyzes Microsoft's new family of seven in-house MAI models, particularly the MAI-Thinking-1 reasoning model. He highlights the strategic framing: offering enterprises the ability to customize and own their models via 'Frontier Tuning' and 'Reinforcement Learning Environments' (RLEs), creating proprietary moats without sharing data with frontier labs. This strategy, he notes, is classic Microsoft: enabling cautious enterprises to adopt AI on their own terms.
