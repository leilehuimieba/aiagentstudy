## Core Takeaway

The article details the author's experience in Baidu's internal R&D environment, leveraging Claude Code's Skill mechanism and three slash commands to fully automate the development of a product feature called "self-evolution." The workflow is divided into three phases: the /prd phase transforms vague requirements into a structured PRD document and automatically splits them into iCafe cards; the /goal phase implements code for each card and automatically writes tests; the /after-goal phase automatically completes code commits, Gerrit pushes, scoring and merging, card updates, and closure. The article summarizes best practices such as prioritizing PRD, controlling card granularity, preferring CLI over browsers, and using Skills as carriers of process knowledge. It also compares this approach with the Autoresearch solution, offering a lightweight, fully automated, and reusable solution for AI-assisted development.
