## Core Takeaway

Based on years of SaaS entrepreneurial consulting experience, author Wu Hao proposes an AI transformation grading system from a business perspective for software companies: L1 Personal Productivity, L2 Process Embedding, L3 Task Execution, and L4 Business Restructuring. The article elaborates on the definition, judgment criteria, and typical scenarios for each level, highlighting that the transition from L2 to L3 is the true watershed, as AI evolves from a process node to a controllable task system, fundamentally altering the company's value delivery model. Using HuiSuanZhang as an in-depth case study, it demonstrates how the company embedded AI into its accounting task system through the 'Lobster AI Assistant' and 'AI Workshop,' achieving a leap from L2 to L3.5. Key characteristics of L3 are distilled: a real task entry point, a complete task loop, reusable capability accumulation, governance and fallback mechanisms, and a shift in human roles. Finally, the article outlines a typical evolution roadmap for software companies from L1 to L4.
