- Browser title: Building a New Paradigm for Project Management in the AI Era - Xiaohongshu PMO Team's Agentic Exploration
- Description: This article details the four iterations of the project management AI Agent by the Xiaohongshu PMO team over the past year. Version 1.0 positioned AI as a project management consultant, enabling Q&A through a knowledge base RAG. Version 2.0 introduced the principle of 'Agent Planning + Sub Agent Execution,' allowing the Agent to connect with internal IM for actions like reminders and summaries. Version 3.0, based on the OpenClaw framework, packaged project management capabilities through Skills and built a long-term memory system to create a personal assistant. Version 4.0 upgraded the architecture to '1 Project × N People × M Sessions,' providing project teams with shared context and role-aware dedicated PMO BPs. The article delves into the core propositions, technical architecture, key insights, and lessons learned at each stage, and summarizes 7 practical principles, including the importance of evaluation sets, atomic Agent closure, structured memory, and prioritizing master data. It concludes with a vision for the future, moving from 'humans managing projects with AI assistance' to 'AI leading, humans supporting.'
