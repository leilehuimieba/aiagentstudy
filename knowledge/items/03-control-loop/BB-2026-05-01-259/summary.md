## Core Takeaway

Ian Thomas from Meta's Horizon Experiences team shares a comprehensive case study on fostering AI native engineering. The journey began in May 2025 with a small, safe group focused on integrating AI tools to reduce toil and enhance engineering excellence. They developed the 'Assess and Grow' framework, a maturity model based on DORA, to help teams self-assess across six dimensions like workflow integration and trust. This was paired with a two-day structured training program. The community grew organically from 10 to over 400 members, and weekly active tool usage rose from 30-40% to over 80%. The talk highlights several success stories, including an unsupervised agent that boosted code coverage to over 90% with only three hours of manual effort, and a supervised approach that halved the time for a complex code migration. Key insights include the importance of a safe space for vulnerability, the need for structured learning over ad-hoc experimentation, and the value of domain-specific tools like MCP servers to bridge knowledge gaps.
