## Core Takeaway

This article from Jane Street's engineering blog announces a significant strategic shift: the company is now building a team focused on formal methods after 25 years of skepticism. The author explains that the emergence of agentic coding has fundamentally changed the cost/benefit calculus. On the cost side, AI models make formal methods easier to use, broadening the pool of productive users. On the benefit side, the 'verification bottleneck' created by AI-generated code (which tends to be 'slop') is more critical than ever, and formal methods can provide powerful feedback for AI agents. The article argues that Jane Street is uniquely positioned due to its deep control over its in-house language, OxCaml, and a community of programmers eager for advanced type-system features. It concludes with a call for applications for formal methods experts in London and New York.
