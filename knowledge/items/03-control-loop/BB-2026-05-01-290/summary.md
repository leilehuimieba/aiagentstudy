## Core Takeaway

This article synthesizes two YC talks on AI-native companies, proposing a radical yet logically consistent company form: AI is not a new engine for an old company, but the operating system for a new one. Core ideas include: companies must be queryable/legible to AI, with all knowledge recorded and structured; shifting from open-loop to closed-loop systems, enabling AI not only to execute tasks but also to detect failures, repair systems, and self-improve; organizational structure evolving from pyramid-style information flow to a company brain with human judgment at the edges; the future competition being about token consumption rather than headcount; and software being temporary, with business context and decision principles as core assets. The article emphasizes that founders must personally use AI to break old cognitive boundaries and truly enter the new paradigm.
