## Core Takeaway

Based on the speech and in-depth interview of Rokid founder Misa at Rokid Open Day, the article comprehensively examines the latest progress in the smart glasses industry. Misa announced that Rokid's sales grew by 800% year-on-year, with products covering 166 countries and 50-60% of activated users wearing them all day, but he believes the industry is still in the BlackBerry era, with the starting gun yet to be fired. He compared the fundamental route differences between China (AI computing device form factor) and overseas (fashion accessory path), emphasizing that supply chain trust relies on product judgment rather than advance payments and betting agreements. Misa frankly stated that a killer app that is both high-frequency and rigid-demand has not yet been found, and expects to face direct competition from tech giants in 2027. He revealed that Rokid's YodaOS is Agent-centric and may adopt a Token-based billing model in the future, but the ecosystem needs 2 million active users to sustain independent developers. The article also covers privacy controversies, the social and commercial value of accessibility, and globalization and localization strategies, presenting the strategic thinking and practical challenges of a startup in the smart glasses field.
