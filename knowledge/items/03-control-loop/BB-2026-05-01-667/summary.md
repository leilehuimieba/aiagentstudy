## Core Takeaway

The article is an in-depth interview by ifanr with Netlink CEO Liu Yu. Netlink is a satellite communication terminal startup dedicated to pushing satellite communication from the B-end/G-end to the C-end market. Liu Yu elaborated on the timing of the startup, industry pain points, product philosophy, and technological breakthroughs. Netlink believes that China's satellite communication resources are being built at a high speed, but the lack of terminals and application ecosystems has led to an unclosed commercial loop. Drawing on their experience in the consumer electronics industry, the team defines products based on user needs, introducing precision manufacturing and industrial design into satellite terminals to achieve miniaturization, low cost, and mass production. Technical highlights include a miniaturized dual-motor transmission structure developed in collaboration with the MacBook hinge team, a self-developed GaN PA chip (reducing costs by three times while leading in performance), and edge computing and AI algorithms to optimize low-bandwidth video transmission. In terms of the market, Netlink focuses on scenario exploration, having provided communication support for the 'Biao Ren' crew in uninhabited areas and events like the 800 Li Desert Extreme Race, while also discovering the need for emergency communication in densely populated urban areas. Liu Yu believes the C-end satellite communication market requires patience, advocating for segmenting target customer groups more finely and gradually moving from prosumer C-end to the mass market, with an annual shipment of 100,000 units being a crucial threshold. The article demonstrates how entrepreneurs use consumer electronics thinking to transform a traditional industry, offering reference value for understanding the C-end landing of commercial aerospace.
