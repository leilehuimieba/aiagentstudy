## Key Evidence

This work focuses on anticipatory reflection: instead of only analyzing mistakes after they happen, the agent forecasts likely failure modes and prepares contingency paths ahead of execution.

## Practical Notes

This is particularly relevant for avoiding premature stopping or low-value repeated attempts in exploratory security tasks.
