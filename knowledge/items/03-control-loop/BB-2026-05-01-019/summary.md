## Core Takeaway

Harness is the system around the model: orchestration, tools, state, verification, and feedback loops. It is especially important for multi-agent collaboration and self-improving workflows.

## Reusable Principle

The model is not the whole agent. The harness determines whether model capability becomes reliable work.
