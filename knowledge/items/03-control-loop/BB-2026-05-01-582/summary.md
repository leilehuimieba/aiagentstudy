## Core Takeaway

This episode from Latent.Space features Zico Kolter and Matt Fredrikson, co-founders of Gray Swan, discussing the current state of AI red teaming. They explain that AI security is not merely 'cybersecurity with AI'—AI systems have inherent, novel vulnerabilities (e.g., prompt injection, adversarial attacks) that require a dedicated security mindset. The conversation covers Gray Swan's two-pronged approach: a community-driven red teaming arena (15,000+ participants) and automated red teaming with their model 'Shade,' which can now outperform human red teamers in controlled experiments. They introduce 'The Lethal Trifecta' (untrusted data, private data, exfiltration) and highlight that frontier models do not automatically become more robust with scale. The guests also discuss agent-native identity, permissions, the inevitability of a major AI prompt-injection breach, and the potential for AI security to become part of insurance and compliance stacks.
